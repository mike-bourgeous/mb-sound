# Master effects

<!-- summary -->
`Session#master` / console `master { |mix| mix.softclip }` runs the mix through a chain of `GraphNode::MixSource` channels; new chains start at launch points with the old one spilling over (`fade:` crossfades), `master nil` bypasses, and the master gain applies before the chain (`post: true` after it).
Without a chain, realtime live output ends in a transparent -1 dBFS `Session::SafetyLimiter` (never in renders, stems, takes, taps).
Mastering presets (`master :club`, `:streaming`, `:quiet`, `:vinyl`; `mix.master(:club)` anywhere; `masters`) are frozen `Mastering::Preset` values: renders reach their loudness target exactly in two passes (`Mastering::Targeting`), live uses the preset's drive or `ride: true`.

Detailed notes moved from CLAUDE.md (2026-10-10); the summary block above feeds the generated index (`design/notes/README.md`, `rake docs:index`).  Update it when behavior changes.


`Session#master` (`lib/mb/sound/session/master.rb`, console `master { |mix| mix.softclip }`) runs the whole mix through a chain built on `GraphNode::MixSource` channels (one param = the mix as a stereo bundle, N params = one per channel; `master nil` bypasses).  New chains start at `bg`-style launch points (`on:`/`in:`/`at:`) (through the change queue, so chains queued for different times each start; for the same time the latest wins; undo rebuilds the previous chain from its block); by default the old chain "spills over" (fed silence from the switch sample so tails ring out, dropped after 1 s below -90 dB or 10 s), `fade:` crossfades, `fade: 0` cuts (also used when the render load is over 60%).  Clips in a chain follow the timeline like players' clips (non-looping and launch-aligned ones count from the chain's start, `Chain#launch`, through seeks and resumes).  Chains keep processing while idle, `panic` rebuilds the chain to clear tails, and `render` adds the tail after the last player (10 s cap).  Nodes that change the sample count (`resample`, `oversample`) can't be used in a master chain yet.  A block written without its parameter (`master { reverb }`, `bus(:verb) { reverb }`) raises one line saying to use `it` or name it (`MB::Sound::BlockHints`; also in patch errors for an unnamed `clip`; omnibus15).  See Reverbs for which reverbs are light enough for a live master chain.

Shared nodes (fixes-12, 2026-10-14): a chain's nodes may share nodes with players (`lfo = 3.hz.lfo; bg :a, lfo * 0.5; master { |mix| mix * (lfo * 0.5 + 1) }`).  Master chains count as Session graphs for the shared-node bookkeeping in `arrangement.md` (`Strip#span_graphs` lists each non-bypass chain's `Chain#chain_graph`, a `ChainGraph` whose `#graph` covers all its nodes): a muted or kept player sharing a node only with a chain still reads its branch, and a chain waiting to start (a queued `:master` change, an undo's master `:restore`, a chain landing in this span) skips its branches of shared nodes to their Tees' leaders at the start of every span (`Strip#catch_up_waiting`), like a waiting player.  A chain starting inside a span is sampled from its start frame since omnibus16 (`Chain#skip`, `Master#render_master_chain_from`; its shared branches catch up with the frames before it, `Strip#catch_up_chain`, as a player's do; before, it read the span from its first frame with silent input or a 0 gain before its start); with `mix * lfo`-style chains the result is exact at any buffer size (`master_spec` 'nodes shared with players': 0.3 s and 1.5 s waits, a replacement while the old chain spills over, a muted player, an undo, at 128/801/7, 800, and 7-frame buffers).  Before, the waiting chain played the node late by its wait (0.3 s: 0.1 off) and its first read after more than a second raised `BranchBufferOverflow` (measured on master-ai 94c421d7); so did unmuting a player that shared a node only with a chain.  Bus chains take part since fixes-13 (`buses.md`, "Shared nodes").

Master gain placement (fixes-6, 2026-10-13; maintainer's option 1): `Session#master_gain` applies before the master chain by default (pre), so a limiter or clipper there keeps its ceiling whatever the gain (before, the gain came after the chain: Loop Church reached +0.76 dBTP with a gain above 0 dB).  `master_gain 2.db, post: true` (`Session#master_gain_post = true`, `Session.new(master_gain_post:)`) puts it back after the chain, like the Pre/Post Fader Insert FX switch in Vegas/ACID; the placement stays until changed (`master_gain post: false`).  Without a master chain both placements give the same samples (spec).  Renders take the current session's placement; set documents save `master_gain x, post: true`; takes save `master_gain_post:` (takes saved before it existed replay post, as they were recorded).  Chain tails count as quiet by the chain's output, so a pre gain changes how long a tail runs by the gain's dB.  Stems are unchanged (each part times the gain).  Always-on master limiting/softclip is for the effects project.

Buses (buses-1, 2026-10-13; `buses.md`) reuse the chain machinery here (`Chain`, `build_master(label:)`, `activate_master`, `render_master_chain`, `master_chain_done?(c, list)`) for named buses with their own inputs; master chains can read buses and players (`mix.duck(:kick)`), bound by `build_master`.

## Warm-ups (dropouts-2, 2026-10-28)

`Master#master` builds the new chain again in a sandbox and plays it under a noise stand-in before queueing the change (`Session::Warming`, arrangement.md): the landing buffer of `master { it.softclip(0, 0.1).*(0.1.hz.lfo.at(-6.db..20.db)).softclip }` went from 48 ms (46 ms YJIT compiling) to 4 ms, ~90 ms more at the prompt.  On the render thread the chains' output buffers are reused (`Master#master_out`; the mix's consumers copy what they keep).  Fixed in omnibus16: a chain landing inside a buffer got zeros for the frames before its start, which primed softclip's ADAA state from 0 instead of the first real sample (8 frames differed, up to -32 dB, at 367- vs 800-frame buffers; dropouts-2's session fuzz seeds 11 and 28): it is now sampled from its start frame (a fading or cutting chain, whose gain was 0 before its start, too: no input before its start reaches its state), its timeline nodes start there, and a mastering first-pass capture records the part from the start frame, so `Mastering::Targeting` replays it alike.  session_fuzz_spec keeps the shrunk song.

## Live safety limiter (omnibus9, 2026-10-18)

The maintainer's pick (queue item 13): a realtime Session with no master chain or preset ends its live output in a transparent safety limiter (`Session::SafetyLimiter`, `lib/mb/sound/session/safety_limiter.rb`): `limit(ceiling: -1.db, lookahead: 0, true_peak: false, release: 0.1)` on a `MixSource` of the mix, linked across channels.  Zero lookahead keeps it latency-free (the gain drops on the sample that would pass -1 dBFS; sample peaks, so about -1 dBTP), so it switches with master chains without a time jump, and below the ceiling it is bit-transparent (spec).  It hears every buffer while on (its gain stays current under a chain) and reaches the output only while `master_active?` is false (old chains ringing out count as active).  Order: mix -> (post-chain master gain) -> safety limiter -> CUE split -> monitor trim (which keeps its own clip warning).  Renders (non-realtime Sessions), stems, takes, `rec_audio`, taps, and captures read the mix before it.  `Session.new(safety_limiter:)` (default: realtime), `#safety_limiter=`, `#safety_limiting?`; `master` (master_info) says `bypass (live output: a safety limiter at -1 dBFS until a master chain is set)`.  Cost: one linked limiter, ~0.4% of realtime per stereo 800-sample buffer (dynamics.md's limiter figure).  Spec: `spec/lib/mb/sound/session/safety_limiter_spec.rb`.

## Mastering presets (mastering-1, 2026-10-17)

Maintainer (2026-10-17, question picker): "Could we get some mastering work done here"; loudness targets reached **both ways** (renders measure, then set the gain exactly, in two deterministic passes; live gets an opt-in slow rider into the limiter); ship all four presets `:club`, `:streaming`, `:quiet`, `:vinyl`.  Standing taste: dance masters low crest and near the maximum true peak, shares at -0.5 dBTP; "less clipping, more limiting" (2026-10-13).  Persona panel round 2 asked for this (#13, #19, H5, `master :quiet`).

**API** (`lib/mb/sound/mastering.rb`, `mastering/targeting.rb`, `mastering/codec_peaks.rb`, `graph_node/rider.rb`, `graph_node/mastering_methods.rb`):
- `master :club` (console and `Session#master(preset, **options, &block)`): a preset ends the master chain; with a block, the block's effects come first (`master(:club) { |mix| mix.filter(:highpass, cutoff: knob(:air)) }`; in short `master(:club) { |mix| fx }`).  Same launch points, spill-over, fades, undo (the chain, its `MasterState`, and `reset_master` carry the preset), journal entries (`master :club, lufs: -9 lands 5:1`).
- `mix.master(:club)` / `mix.master(:club, ...)` (alias `mastering`) applies a preset anywhere in a graph (recorded by recipes: `.master(:streaming, platform: :apple)`); `mix.ride(-14, ...)` the rider alone.
- Options (`Mastering::OPTIONS`; `lufs: platform: ceiling: drive: ride: mono: glue: clip: de_ess:`): `lufs:` (LUFS, a `Loudness::TARGETS` name, or a Target), `platform:` (the same, by name), `ceiling:` (true peak, `-1.db`), `drive:` (a fixed trim: renders keep it), `ride:` (true or rider settings), `mono:` (Hz or false), `glue:`, `clip:` (club), `de_ess:` (vinyl crossover Hz or false).  Unknown names raise with did_you_mean.
- `Mastering::Preset` is a frozen Data value: `steps` ([method, args, kwargs], one `[:trim]` gain stage), `target` (Loudness::Target), `ceiling`, `limiter`, `drive`, `fixed`, `ride`, `options`, `notes`, `source`; `#call_ruby` (`:club, lufs: -9`), `#to_ruby`, `#summary`.  `masters` lists them; `masters(:vinyl).notes`.
- Set documents save `master :club, lufs: -9` (and `master(:quiet) { |mix| ... }`), and load both.  Takes replay the typed line.  Genre templates may name a preset as a master step (`master :club, lufs: -8`; the template's `mono_below` becomes the preset's `mono:`).  Song scripts: `--master PRESET|none` (`Session#mastering_override`: replaces presets the song's `master` calls give, is added after plain master blocks; `none` drops presets) and `--lufs N|name`; `genre_demo.rb -M club` swaps a template's chain for a preset.

**The trim.**  Every chain has one gain stage after the level-independent stages (mono low end, subsonic highpass) and before everything that reacts to level, so glue, clipper, and limiter see about the same level whatever the mix: thresholds are written relative to the target (`glue_step(target, over:)`).  Its value is:
- in renders, measured (below);
- live, the preset's `drive`: target minus `ASSUMED_INPUT_LUFS` (-20 LUFS, about where full-scale parts land at the default -10 dB master gain); or
- with `ride: true`, a `GraphNode::Rider` starting there.

**Render targeting** (`Mastering::Targeting`): `render` runs its block twice when a targeted preset ran.  Pass 1 renders normally while a `Capture` records the Session's mix (a tap) and, for each chain with a targeted preset, its input, its output times its fade ramp, and the root random generator's state when it was built.  `Targeting.solve` rebuilds those chains on the closed pass-1 Session (same block, same seeds, `Session#mastering_trim` set) and computes the render's output for any trim as the recorded mix minus the chains' recorded outputs plus their outputs at that trim (so other chains, spill-overs, fades, and post gains are exact).  Secant steps on the BS.1770 integrated loudness from the window's first frame (`from:` renders) reach the target within 0.01 LU in 2-4 chain runs; presets without a limiter (`:vinyl`) then lower the trim until the true peak is under the ceiling ("held").  Pass 2 re-renders with the trim (the root generator put back first).  Everything is sample-exact, so the trim and the file are bit-identical at any buffer size (spec: 128 vs 800).  The block must build its graphs: a graph pass 1 already played raises (`Capture#saw`).  `render(loudness:)` overrides the preset's target (without a preset it still normalizes the file, gain only); `loudness: false` renders once at the drive; `render(master: :club)` masters any render block (`render(master:, loudness:)`; `loudness: false` skips targeting once).  Stems are pre-master, so the trim leaves them alone.  The printed report (`Targeting::Result`, `Targeting.last`; inside `MixReport` with `report:`): pass/fail, integrated vs target, short-term max, LRA, PLR, true peak vs ceiling, side below the mono frequency (or 120 Hz) re mid, decoded MP3/AAC true peaks (streaming presets and reports) with ceiling advice over -0.1 dBTP.

**Rider** (`GraphNode::Rider`, a Channels bundle like LoudnessMeter): K-weighted mean square per sample; 10 ms segments filled sample by sample (sums independent of buffer boundaries); every 100 ms of its own time it reads the short-term loudness (last 3 s, silence before the start), moves the gain (dB) a one-pole step toward target minus loudness (time constants `down:` 3 s when the program gets louder, `up:` 10 s when quieter), within `range:` (9 dB) of the start, and ramps there linearly over the next 100 ms (gain a function of the absolute sample index).  It holds below -70 LUFS and while the input is more than `gate:` (10 LU) under the recent loudest short-term loudness (a peak falling 0.5 LU/s), so silence, fades, and breakdowns don't pump up.  It measures its input (feed-forward), so after heavy limiting the output lands a little under the target.  Same samples at 1/7/128/4801-sample buffers as at 800 (spec).  `master` shows its gain: `master: :club, ride: true (ride +2.4 dB)`.

**Codec true peaks** (`Mastering.codec_peaks(file | channels, codecs:)`): ffmpeg encodes (MP3 320k, AAC 256k, Opus 160k, Vorbis q9), decodes, and measures BS.1770 Annex 2 true peak.  AAC encodes with libfdk_aac, else aac_at (ffmpeg on macOS), else is nil (`aac n/a` with a note in reports, out of the ceiling advice); `:aac_ffmpeg` measures ffmpeg's built-in encoder on request (`CodecPeaks.encoders` lists what ffmpeg has).  Why (aac-peaks, 2026-10-23): the built-in `aac` encoder (ffmpeg 7.1, the container's; no libfdk_aac there) puts 3-6 ms noise bursts into hot, bright masters, louder than the music in every band from 3 kHz up (Tunnel Vision `:hot` at 120.48 s: error up to 1.1 against a 0.87 source sample, error RMS -12 dBFS over 128 samples), so decoded peaks read +5.9 dBTP on that render (+4.2 to +12.2 across the decks-1 renders, dnb +3.2) against +1.0 for MP3; on a 25 s excerpt AAC +3.6, MP3 +0.7, Vorbis +0.5, Opus +0.6 dBTP.  Not a measurement error: ffmpeg's `ebur128=peak=true` agrees with our meter within 0.1 dB on every decode, the decodes are float and aligned (lag 0), and a faded-in -6 dBFS sine reads -6.0 after every codec (spec).  Every built-in variant overshoots (128k +3.4, 192k +4.5, 320k +7.3, `-aac_coder fast` +2.5, PNS/TNS/IS/MS off +3.1, `-cutoff 16000` +2.6), and the other MDCT codecs stay within about 1 dB, the overshoot real encoders give, which the existing ceiling advice covers; so no codec headroom stage is needed for AAC.

**The chains** (`Mastering.preset(...).steps`; thresholds relative to the target because the trim brings the mix there first):

| preset | chain | target, ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| `:club` | `mono_below(120)` -> trim -> glue `compress(threshold: target + 3 dB, ratio: 2, knee: 6, attack: 10 ms, release: 150 ms, detector: :rms)` -> `softclip(0.9, 1.4)` (`clip: false`: `limit(ceiling: ceiling + 2 dB, lookahead: 1 ms, release: 20 ms)`) -> `limit(ceiling: -0.5.db, release: 80 ms)` | -8.5 LUFS, -0.5 dBTP |
| `:streaming` | trim -> glue (target + 5 dB, 1.5:1, 20/250 ms, RMS) -> `limit(ceiling: platform's, release: 150 ms)` | `platform:`/`lufs:` (default `:spotify` -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP; `:apple_music` -16, `:amazon_music` -2 dBTP, ...) |
| `:quiet` | trim -> `limit(ceiling: -1.db, release: 250 ms)` (`glue: true` adds 1.3:1 at target + 6 dB) | -18 LUFS, -1 dBTP; `lufs: :ebu_r128` -23, `:atsc_a85` -24 / -2 dBTP |
| `:vinyl` | `mono_below(150)` -> 25 Hz highpass -> trim -> glue (target + 5 dB, 1.5:1, 30/300 ms, RMS) -> de-esser `bands(5500) { |lo, hi| [lo, hi.compress(threshold: target - 8 dB, ratio: 4, attack: 1 ms, release: 60 ms)] }`; no limiter | -14 LUFS, true peak held under -1 dBTP by the trim |

Reasons: club and vinyl mono the lows (club systems and cutting lathes; genres.md's decision, 2026-10-13: never for headphone or ambient masters, so streaming and quiet don't); glue is gentle and RMS-detected so it evens sections rather than catching transients; the club's peak stage was chosen by measurement (below) for "less clipping, more limiting"; quiet keeps dynamics (EBU R 128 programmes have LRA 5-20 LU); vinyl has no brickwall because the cutting engineer sets the cut level, and tames sibilance because the RIAA pre-emphasis (about +20 dB at 20 kHz re 1 kHz) makes treble cost groove excursion (sources in each preset's `source`; the RIAA and cutting advice from memory, unverified).

## Mastering round 2 (mastering-2, 2026-10-17/18)

Maintainer's Bench verdicts on round 1: dnb and techno pick club without the clipper ("The IMD on some of the stabs is noticeable in the clipping variant"), house keeps the gentle clipper ("The two limiter setup isn't much different so both are good to have"), drums_808 non-clipping; Tunnel Vision keeps its own master ("Need a way to squeeze just a little more loudness out of the limiters without hitting dangerous true peaks levels"); Memory Card keeps its own ("It's hard to give up the treble that's lost in the vinyl preset even at matched levels"); Night Bus streaming at Apple's -16, Tin Roof and the ambient template quiet, the wavetable song streaming.  Then: "Option for the mastering: dynamic eq so air is only cut near clips, multiband dynamics to similar end" and "The peak limiting dynamics would probably need to operate on a side chain of the full mix if only touching the air band".

Changes: `:club` defaults to `clip: false` (two limiters: 1 ms / 20 ms at the ceiling + 2 dB, then 1.5 ms / 60 ms at -0.5 dBTP; was 80 ms) after an `air:` stage (default `:static`, a 4th-order Butterworth lowpass at 18 kHz; `:dynamic` the band above 12 kHz cut 20:1 while the full mix is over the ceiling, `:dynamic_air` step, the maintainer's sidechain design; `:band` that band compressed on its own level, `:air_band`; `false`).  Vinyl's de-esser moved to 7 kHz, target - 4 dB, 3:1, 40 ms.  Templates (dnb: air highpass then `master :club`; techno `master :club`; house `master :club, clip: true`; ambient `master :quiet`) and songs (drums_808 club, Night Bus streaming at -16 with its own highpass and softclip first, Tin Roof quiet, wavetable streaming) use presets; `tunnel_vision.rb --club -7.6` and `memory_card.rb --vinyl` tried the presets on the two songs that kept their own masters (both default to them since omnibus9: Tunnel Vision club at -8.5 on the loudest 8 bars, Memory Card vinyl with rebalanced drums).  Targeting stops climbing once the limiters saturate (under 0.05 LU per dB of trim) and reports FAIL.

Honest masters and per-section trims (decks-1, 2026-10-22; the maintainer's "fixes only" mastering round: Tunnel Vision's snares distorting before the breakdown, Bench 2026-10-19, and item D of the panel round 3 ballot: "per-section targets that keep the energy curve, `:vinyl` that clips first, errors on ignored options, side measured before mono_below, per-medium targets"):
- Options a preset would ignore raise (Nev's N14: `master(:vinyl, clip: true)` was accepted and did nothing).  `Mastering::COMMON_OPTIONS` go to every preset, `PRESET_OPTIONS` (`club: clip air`, `vinyl: clip de_ess`) to their own; `loudest_bars:` needs `measure: :loudest`, `percentile:` `:bars`, `energy:` `:sections`.  The error names the presets that take the option: `master :streaming would ignore clip: (an option of :club and :vinyl)`.
- `:vinyl, clip: true` (Munro's M9, Nev's N12: a 909 kick or a break held the trim at the -1 dBTP ceiling, -18 LUFS for a -14 target): a `softclip` last in the chain, its limit `VINYL_CLIP_MARGIN` (0.5 dB) under the ceiling for true peaks between samples, its knee at half the limit (-6 dB), so the render's trim reaches the target with the kick's peaks rounded off.  Off by default (a default change waits for the Bench).  Spec: a kick-led mix held 2+ LU short of -8 LUFS reaches it within 0.05 LU with true peak under -1 dBTP.  On by default since 2026-10-22 (maintainer: "vinyl that clips first"; `clip: false` for trim alone).
- Side before the chain: targeted reports print the side below the mono crossover in the mix entering the chain as well as mastered (`Result#side_in`; `Targeting.input_from` concatenates the chain's recorded inputs); and `mono_below` itself no longer changes mono content (dynamics.md, N16).
- `measure: :sections` with `energy:` (0..1, default `SECTION_ENERGY` 1): each pass of the song form's sections gets its own trim.  The first pass records where each section pass starts (`Capture#note_sections`, called from `render_master_chain` with the span's first transport position: master chains render after the transport advanced, and the transport's position then put the starts one buffer early, so the trims differed by buffer size until fixed; `MixReport::SectionMarks`, shared with MixReport's section table) and each pass's loudness in the chain's input.  `.solve` first finds the uniform trim that brings the loudest pass to `lufs:`, then `.solve_sections` gives every pass within `BARS_RANGE` (20 LU) of the loudest the target `lufs + energy x (its mix level - the loudest's)` and moves each pass's trim by secant steps on its own loudness through the chain (`SECTION_STEPS` 6, `SECTION_TOLERANCE` 0.05 LU; each step one run of the chain over the whole song); quieter passes take the trim before them, and no trim goes more than `SECTION_MAX_LIFT` (6 dB) over the loudest pass's.  The trims become a `Targeting::TrimCurve` (dB at session frames, linear in dB between points): each change ramps over a quarter note toward the lower trim first, so a louder section's trim is reached by its first frame and a quieter one's starts rising there (loud material never plays at a quiet section's trim).  `TrimCurve::Node` multiplies at the trim stage reading `Session#frame` (or `frame_override` while `.solve` replays recorded spans), so the second pass is the same at any buffer size (spec'd at 800 and 333, the common length: file lengths still differ by a sample, F8).  energy 1 keeps the mix's distances (Kez's K22, "raise the quiet bars by trim, never by the clipper"); less narrows them.
