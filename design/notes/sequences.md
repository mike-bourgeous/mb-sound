# Sequences

<!-- summary -->
`lib/mb/sound/sequence/`: immutable `Clip`s of `Event`s in exact Rational whole notes, built with `seq(C4, E4, G4.n4).n8`, chord steps, `grid('x...x...')`, and note length methods; a clip is a MIDI source (`clip.stream`, `clip.notes`, `clip.synth(voices:) { |v, i| }`) whose edges land on exact samples at the shared `Transport`'s tempo.  Transforms (legato, transpose, ratchet, swing, humanize, chance, groove, ghost chords, ...) share their cores with the MIDI stream transforms (`Sequence::Feel`) and run per row on grid Kits (`rotate` wraps, `shift` drops); `clip.show` draws them as text; clips carry controller lanes and note marks (accent `!A1`, slide `~A1`, flam), remember what plays them (`Sequence::PlayedBy`), and `swap` changes a player's clips on the next bar.  `!` is overridden only on Note and Step: test with `nil?`, never `!x`.

At a glance (forms the sections below describe at length):

- Building: `seq(C4, E4, G4.n4).n8`, chord steps `seq([C3, E3, G3], chord(:min7, root: :d))`, `grid('x...x...')` (division 16 unless a division is given first: `grid(8, 'x.x.')`), note length methods (`n4`, `.d`, `.t`).  `clip.synth(voices:) { |v, i| }` plays a clip on a Synth.
- Transforms: `legato`, `note_length`, `reverse`, `permute`, `rotate` (wraps) / `shift` (drops what leaves the clip), `transpose(step, scale:, root:)`, `ratchet(n, rate:)`, `halftime`/`doubletime` (clips, Seqs, Kits); feel and chance: `humanize`, `quantize`, `swing`, `chance`/`maybe`, `every`, `Clip::Variation`s (`vary: true`), `bake(fx)`; generators `euclid`, `melody`.  Clip transforms work per cycle on generator clips (`map_clip`, `map_cycles`).  Clips move notes both ways and wrap loops; streams only delay (midi.md).
- Edges: looping clips follow the timeline (`align: :launch` counts from the launch); launches and swaps land on the floor sample of their time; reads no edge can reach compare integer frames against the next edge's frame (`ClipSource` quiet reads, midi.md).
- Controller lanes: `events(..., controls: { cc1: [[pos, v], ...] })`, `clip.controls`, `#fit_controls` (cubic/linear keys sent every 1/512 whole note); kept by note transforms, moved by time transforms, played before notes at the same time, and chased after launches, seeks, swaps, and skips.
- Rhythm and feel templates (help `:templates`): `clip.rhythm.notes(C1, F1, [A1, E2], nil)` / `.pitches(0, 2, 4, scale:)` pour pitches into a clip's hits; `take.groove` measures per-16th offsets and levels (`grid:`, `triplets:`); `beat.groove(feel, amount:, velocity:)` moves notes onto them (`Feel.groove_target`); `Stream#groove` delays live notes the same way.
- 303 patterns: `Seq#acid` and `acid(...)`; voice helpers `v.accent`, `v.accent_sweep`, `v.acid_env`; demo `bin/synths/acid.rb`, `bin/songs/acid_song.rb`.
- Ghost chords: `clip.ghost_chords(1, 4, 7, 6, per: 1.bar, scale: scale(:minor, :a), harmonic:, tie: :contour)` snaps a repeated melody to a silent progression (degrees from 1, note Arrays, nil), keeping rhythm and contour; looping clips repeat to fit; without `scale:` the chords are in the session key.  Scale methods count degrees from 0, `ghost_chords` from 1.
- What plays a clip (`Sequence::PlayedBy`): `brk.chop(...)` clips play their slices (`bg :x, chop` / `play chop` = `chop.sampler(brk)`), `machine.grid` grids their kit design; plain clips play a soft saw, grids a TR-909 (a TR-808 when they name 808-only voices; omnibus9), envelopes a triangle plus pink noise (`Playable.graph`); `clip.played_by(sf2(...))` picks one.
- Swaps: `swap :bass, bass2` changes a player's clips on the next bar, keeping graph state; derived clips are rederived; `swap :drums, kit2` swaps a grid Kit's rows by name (rows it lacks go silent, rows the graph has no voices for warn; since omnibus13).  Demos `bin/songs/sequence_demo.rb`, `swap_song.rb`.


`lib/mb/sound/sequence/` (`MB::Sound::Sequence`) holds musical sequences: immutable `Clip`s of `Event`s timed in exact Rational whole notes, built with `seq` (e.g. `seq(C4, E4, G4.n4).n8`), `grid` (drum step strings like `'x...x...'`), and note length methods on `Note` (`n1`-`n8`, `n12`-`n128`, `.d`, `.t`, long names).  A clip is just a MIDI source: `clip.stream` is a `MIDI::Stream` of a new `MIDI::ClipSource` (a `TimelineNode`; a new source per call, since each follows its own graph's timeline), and its node methods are the mono (last-note) `Notes` nodes of `clip.notes`: `clip.env`/`amp_env`/`fm_env`/`filt_env` (NoteEnvelopes: `:analog` curves, default 5 ms/0.2/0.7/0.3, Envelope keywords such as `curve:`/`sensitivity:` (velocity range; `velocity:` is the input), GM2 time scaling off unless `gm: true`), `clip.tone`/`hz` (a key-synced NotePitch: `clip.tone.saw`, `.transpose(7)`), `clip.freq` (Hz node), `clip.period`, `clip.gate`, `clip.trigger`, `clip.number`, `clip.velocity(range:)`; each call makes its own Notes, so share one with `n = clip.notes`.  Edges land on exact samples at the tempo of a shared `Transport` (`bpm 120`); `spec/test_data/clip_node_reference.json` (outputs of the removed `ClipNode` renderers) is the edge reference.  `clip.synth(voices: 2, **synth_options) { |v, i| }` is `Synth.new(clip, voices:, tail: 0)` (`v` is a lane's Notes; returns Channels for multichannel voices).  Non-looping clips end one buffer after their last note-off; NoteEnvelopes end once their stream ended and they are idle; an ended reset input (key sync trigger) only stops resets, so tones ring through releases.  `legato(0.85)` shortens notes without changing the rhythm.  `reverse` (alias `retrograde`) mirrors a clip or reverses a Seq's steps; `permute([2, 0, 1])` / `permute(seed: 3)` (alias `shuffle`) moves notes among the same rhythm, repeatably from the clip's seed.  In a `Session`, looping clips play in phase with the transport timeline (`seek`, `rewind`), so graphs started at different times stay in sync.  `clip.loop(align: :launch)` (`Clip#launch_aligned?`) instead counts cycles from the exact launch time (`Player#launch`, the bar line rather than the floored first sample; `TimelineNode#start_at(launch:)`), or from the swap that brings it in; seeks and rewinds keep that anchor (the loop plays as the timeline loop rotated by the launch time; before the anchor it plays the cycles leading up to it), only a new launch (`bg`, `resume`) moves it, and `:clip` launch points ignore such loops.  `rotate(amount)` moves a clip's events later by a Duration (or signed whole notes), wrapping within its length; `shift(amount)` moves them without wrapping (parity-1, maintainer 2026-10-30: "rol/ror/shl/shr act like the assembly instructions"): events whose start leaves 0...length drop out and the vacated space is empty, the length stays, controller lanes move alike (a lane moved earlier holds its value at the new start).  Both share `Clip.offset_of`; Seqs inherit them (results are Clips), grid Kits apply them per row.  See `bin/songs/sequence_demo.rb`.  Launches, replacements, swaps, and master chain switches land on the sample whose window holds their time (floor, like Notes edges), so a clip launched on a bar line between samples (e.g. 112 BPM) keeps its first note. See `bin/songs/sequence_demo.rb`.

Note marks (`Sequence::NoteMarks` on Note, the same methods on `Seq::Step`, each returning a Step, chaining in any order: `~!A1`, `A1.!.~`, `(!A1).up.n8`): flam `D2.flam` / `.flam(25.ms)` (grid 'f'/'F'; drum kits play a grace stroke then the hit; see drums.md), accent `!A1`/`.accent`/`.acc`/`.a!` (Step flag `accented`, velocity `Clip::ACCENT_VELOCITY` 1.0 vs default 0.75; grid `X` sets it too), slide `~A1`/`.slide(overlap)`/`.s!` (flag `slid`; the note sounds `Seq::SLIDE_LEGATO` 1.02 of its last step, overlapping the next so mono voices glide and legato envelopes hold; `legato` transforms leave slides alone), octaves `up(n)`/`dn(n)` (alias `down`)/`oct(n)` defined once on Pitch via `transpose` (also on Steps and Seqs).  `!` is overridden only on Note and Step (audit and guard: `spec/lib/mb/sound/note_negation_spec.rb`; test values that may be Notes with `nil?`, never `!x`); `!A1.n8` is `!(A1.n8)` (seq raises with a hint); `!~A1` is Ruby's not-match operator (write `~!A1`); `-A1`/`+A1` stay undefined (minus is for signal inversion).  `Rest`/`R` (a rest Step) and `Tie`/`T` (holds the previous note one more step, resolved when the Seq builds events; a rest after a rest or at the start; grid `-`).  `Seq#acid(accent: 1.0, normal: 0.6, gate: 0.5, slide: 1.02)` plays a Seq like the 303's sequencer (half-step gates, ties hold, slides overlap, accent/normal velocities; tracked for swaps); `acid(*items, step: 16)` is `seq(...).len(step).acid`.  `Seq#loop` returns a looping Seq, so step transforms (`acid`, `acc`, `len`, ...) work on loops.  Voice helpers: `v.accent(0.8)` (0/1 per note from velocity), `v.accent_sweep(env, resonance:)` (the 303's accent capacitor: lowpass at 2 - 0.8 r Hz, x1.6; three accented 16ths at 128 BPM peak 0.57, 0.78, 0.86), `v.acid_env(decay:)` (legato MEG; exponential, curve 30 (`Notes::ACID_ENV_CURVE`), times ×2.6 a linear fall's with the same half-way point (`ACID_ENV_TIME_SCALE`; user's pick 2026-10-10, "the longer multiple for equal half works better for these short notes"): decay 1.56 s, accents 0.52 s (0.6/0.2 linear); `curve: :linear, decay: 0.6, accent_decay: 0.2` is the earlier MEG; `acid_voice(env_curve:)` the same; A/B in /app/tmp/listening/omnibus2/).  Demo voice `bin/synths/acid.rb` (`acid_voice(clip_or_midi, ..., env_curve:)`, a one-voice Synth), song `bin/songs/acid_song.rb`.  Diode accuracy (2026-10-10, study on branch `research-acid-diode`): our equal-capacitor ladder is within 0.7 dB of Stinchcombe's TB-303 core (bottom capacitor halved) at the same knob; the 303's feedback coupling capacitors (~10 Hz) only change the subsonic band (< 0.5 dB at 55 Hz); per-stage diode saturation not built (~2x the diode's cost, a user decision).

`swap :bass, bass2` (`Session#swap`, `lib/mb/sound/session/clip_swaps.rb`) changes a player's clips on the next bar via `ClipSource#swap_clip` on every ClipSource in the graph (exact time; graph and effect state kept; sounding notes get note-offs, held values chase the new clip).  Swaps wait in the Session's change queue and are worked out from the clips playing when they land, so several swaps for one player at different times all happen (a ClipSource holds one pending swap; before 2026-10-10 the later swap silently replaced an earlier queued one); errors that are clear when the swap is made still raise then.  Transformed clips remember their `source` and transform (`Clip#lineage` / `#rederive`), so clips derived from the old one (`bass.transpose(12)`) are rebuilt from the new one, and a `clip.synth` follows through its one source; pass `old => new` pairs (or `kit => kit2` for `grid` rows) when a player has unrelated clips, or a Kit alone (omnibus13; the maintainer asked for kits like scenes take them): each played row (the earliest clip in a source's lineage that was a kit row, by the row name it had there: `Sequence::Kit.row_name`, a WeakMap filled when Kits are made) plays the new kit's row of that name, rows the new kit lacks get a silent loop that keeps the row name (so a later kit brings them back), and rows only the new kit has warn (no voices for them; scenes build the patch again there).  A swap into a player whose clips are all one-shots that have ended, or will have by the swap's time (`ClipSource#over_by?`/`#over_from_by?`, `Session#clips_over_by?`; queued starts too), raises `Session::ClipsOver` (the voices reading an ended clip end for good, so the swap would land unheard and the player end with its tail); Arrangement tracks build their patch again instead (`bg :bass, bass.loop` after `bg :bass, bass` rang out, omnibus15; a session fuzz step re-bgs a looped clip after a one-shot).  See `bin/songs/swap_song.rb`.

Clip feel, chance, and generators (`sequence/clip_feel.rb`, `sequence/generators.rb`; clips move notes both ways, live streams only delay): `humanize(1.n64, velocity: 0.2, seed: clip seed, vary:)`, `quantize(16, amount:, ends:, window:)`, `swing(0.58, 16)` (a time warp per pair of steps), `chance(p)` (alias `maybe`) and `every(n, from: 1)` (Elektron-style cycle conditions, `Event#condition`; also step marks `E4.every(2, from: 2)`, `G4.maybe(0.5)`, and on Seqs; `Clip#plays?` decides both per cycle).  Fill marks (Elektron's FILL condition, 2026-10-10, `arrangement.md`): `E4.fill`, `E4.fill(:big)`, `.nofill(name = nil)` on Notes and Steps, `Seq#fill`/`#nofill`, `Clip#fill` with no argument or a Symbol (`fill(span)` still repeats to fill a span), `Clip#nofill`, `Kit#fill(name, rows or a block returning a Kit)` (layers fill rows onto rows of the same name, looping again), `Kit#nofill(*rows, name:)`; stored as `Event#fill_mark`/`Step#fill_mark` (`[:fill or :nofill, name]`; a Struct member named `fill` would be shadowed by the mark methods).  Marked notes play by the transport's `FillMap` (set by a playing song form) at their note-on (`Clip#edges(fills:)`); without a form, fill notes never play and nofill notes always do.  `Clip::Variation`s (`#variations`, `#events_for(cycle)`) change a loop every cycle repeatably from seed + cycle (starts relative to the cycle, up to a cycle outside it: an early note plays at the end of the previous cycle, cycle 0's and a launched/seeked/swapped-to cycle's early notes at the read start, `Clip#edges(early:)`; loops are humanized this way, `vary: false` too, so downbeats nudged early play exactly once; 2026-10-10: wrapping within the cycle lost or doubled them): `permute(vary: true)`, `humanize(vary: true)`, `melody(vary: true)`; mapping transforms keep them, `#repeat` unrolls them (and conditions) per copy, `|`/`&` take cycle 0.  `bake(fx or block, cycles: 1)` runs MIDI stream transforms offline into a Clip (loops bake their steady state, so echoes wrap; `cycles:` keeps several cycles of a varying loop; code note: may change with library/live work).  Generators: `euclid(hits, steps, note = kick, step: 16, rotate:, velocity:)` (Bjorklund, a Seq) and `melody(scale, count, step:, rhythm: clip, leap: 3, range:, weights:, rest:, seed: (drawn from MB::Sound), vary:)` (a weighted walk over scale degrees).

Ghost chords (`sequence/ghost_chords.rb`, 2026-10-11, sound-design-1; from the maintainer's melody reference): `clip.ghost_chords(1, 4, 7, 6, per: 1.bar, scale: scale(:minor, :a), size: 3, harmonic: false, tie: :contour, passing: nil)` moves each note to the nearest tone (any octave) of a silent chord progression, one chord per `per` (default one bar of the transport's meter), cycling; rhythm, velocities, and marks stay.  Chords are scale degrees counted from 1 like Roman numerals (triads, or `size: 4` sevenths, from every other scale note), explicit Arrays of notes, or nil (no snapping in that span).  `scale:`/`root:` as in the MIDI transforms (a Scale, name, or offsets; default the session key's scale, `key :a, :minor`, read when the chords are built, on `root:` if given: maintainer decision 2026-10-18 in midi-consol-2b, :minor before; every repo caller already passed `scale:`, so renders are unchanged).  `harmonic: true` raises the scale's minor seventh for the chords (harmonic minor: V major, vii diminished; raises without a minor seventh).  Ties between two chord tones follow `tie:` :contour (the direction the original melody moved into the note; repeats and a non-looping clip's first note go down; a looping clip's first note compares with its last), :down, or :up.  Notes shorter than `passing:` stay as passing notes.  A looping clip repeats to the least common multiple of its length and the progression (at most `Clip::MAX_GHOST_REPEATS` = 64 copies) and keeps looping; a non-looping clip keeps its length.  Tracked as a derivation (swaps rederive it).  Streams: `Stream#ghost_chords` (midi-consol-1) counts the timeline's bars, live input included (see the consolidation notes below).  Recipes print it, and the writer now prints Scales as `scale(:minor, A4)` (loadable: `scale` is in `GraphIO::Registry::TOP_EXTRA`) and `Rest`/`Tie` steps (they printed FIXMEs before).
- Explicit events (graph-io-3): `events([0r, 1/8r, 57, 0.75], [1/4r, 1/8r, 60, 1.0, { accented: true }], length: 1/2r, loop: true)` (`SequenceMethods#events`) makes a clip of rows `[start, length, value, velocity, { marks }]`; graph documents and `to_ruby` print clips without a recipe (captures, `Clip.new`) this way (graph_io.md).

Clip and stream consolidation (midi-consol-1, 2026-10-16; maintainer decisions 1-7 of that day): "shared core, two front ends".
- Half and double time (omnibus8, 2026-10-17; panel Kez #16, help H3): `clip.halftime(factor = 2)` / `doubletime(factor = 2)` (aliases `half_time`, `double_time`) are `stretch(factor)` / `stretch(1 / factor)`: every start, length, and the clip (loop) length, exact Rationals; looping clips keep looping and their alignment, so a half-time loop is as long as the stretched clip and follows the timeline on exact samples across loop edges.  Also on Seqs (kept as Seqs, through `Seq#stretch`) and grid Kits (`Kit#stretch`/`#halftime`/`#doubletime`, every row).  They live with `stretch` on Clip rather than in `Sequence::Feel` (they map times uniformly; no per-note draws).  No stream version: slowing or speeding a live stream needs delaying or anticipating its input (maintainer 2026-10-17: not now; maybe some day as a MIDI looper or delay-buffer playback-rate feature; listed in midi.md's not-built list).  Specs: `spec/lib/mb/sound/sequence/halftime_spec.rb`.
- One event-level core per concept, `Sequence::Feel` (`sequence/feel.rb`): `humanize_draw` (two draws per note, timing then velocity), `quantize_step` (`:nearest` for clips, `:next` for streams, `window:`), `swing_warp`, `condition_plays?`, and the defaults `HUMANIZE_TIME` (4 ms), `SWING` (0.58), `GRID` (16).  Clip methods and stream transforms take the same option names: `humanize(time, velocity:, seed:)` (clips also `vary:`; live notes have no cycles), `quantize(grid, amount:, ends:, window:)`, `swing(amount, grid)` (streams from 0.5: they only delay), `transpose(step, scale:, root:)`, `ratchet(count, rate:, velocity:)`, `chance(p)`/`maybe`, `every(n, from:)`, `legato`, `ghost_chords(...)`.  Results of existing calls are unchanged (same arithmetic in the same order; every song and synth render md5-identical).
- Clips may move notes earlier and wrap in loops; streams only delay, so: stream `legato` of 1 or more delays note-offs, below 1 it shortens notes that carry a length hint (`MIDI::Event#length_hint`, round 2 below; a length gives `note_length`), stream `ratchet` needs `rate:` (hits while held, the last ending with the key; clips split the note evenly without one), stream `every` counts bars of the timeline (meter map) where clips count loop cycles, stream `chance` decides per note (seed + counter) where clips decide per cycle and note, stream `ghost_chords` counts chords from the timeline's bars (or `per:` from timeline 0, so timeline-aligned loops get the clip's chords) and has no `passing:`; it works on live input too (round 2: the maintainer lifted decision 3's `live_error`, since the stream follows the timeline's bars).  Equivalence specs (`spec/lib/mb/sound/midi/transform/clip_ops_spec.rb`): a clip baked through the stream op equals the clip op for swing, ratchet with a rate, legato, ghost chords, and scale transposes.
- Transpose (decision 2): `Clip#transpose`/`Seq#transpose(step, scale:, root:)` move by scale degrees (`Transform::Steps`, `Steps#apply_value` keeps Notes as Notes); the default chromatic scale runs the old code, Intervals stay semitones, Arrays add up; pitch-marked sampler hits move their mark by semitones (other scales raise).
- `Transform::Spec` chains carry the clip operations (decision 3): `Stream::TRANSFORMS` lists them, so Notes mirrors them and graph documents load them; a chain must still start with a top-level builder (`echo`, `arp`, `strum`, `chord`) to print as Ruby.
- Length hints (round 2, maintainer 2026-10-16: "length hints, else pass + live_error"): sources and transforms that know a note's length at its note-on put it on the note-on as `Event#length_hint` (in-process only; MIDI bytes unchanged): clip sources (a `Duration` from the note-on to the note's end, so it follows tempo changes), the arpeggiator (gate length), gated echoes, ratchet hits, `note_length`, and `legato` (scaled); `with_note`/`at` copy it (chords, ungated echoes, transpose, humanize, which shifts note-offs with note-ons), and NoteMap transforms that move note-offs (`quantize(ends:)`, `swing`) recompute it through `note_off_time`.  `Stream#legato` below 1 plans the note-off at the note-on from the hint (no added latency): a re-strike keeps the new note (the ledger drops the old planned note-off), an earlier played note-off still ends the note, planned note-offs go out after the stream ends and ring on through `:ring` jumps.  Notes without a hint pass through unchanged after one `live_error` (an error in scripts and specs).
- Naming (decision 6; rotate/shift replaced by parity-1, 2026-10-30): `rotate` wraps (clips, Seqs, Kits; `Stream#rotate` and `Transform::Spec#rotate` raise through `live_error` with a did-you-mean for `shift`, since a live stream can't wrap), `shift` shifts (clips both ways, dropping what leaves; streams only delay, negative amounts raise); graph documents before format 6 load clip `shift` as `rotate`, its old alias; `Clip#legato`/`Seq#legato` take a length for fixed lengths (new `Clip#note_length`; `Seq#len` stays the step length setter, so clips have no `len`), `Stream#legato` a fraction (1 or more) or a length.  Degree bases: Scale methods count from 0, `ghost_chords` from 1 (Roman numerals), transform steps are relative moves (0 = the same note); top-level `chord(:min7, root: :d)` is a chord value, every other `chord(...)` the transform.
- Kit transform parity (parity-1, 2026-10-30; backlog 12.5): grid Kits have `reverse` (`retrograde`), `rotate`, `shift`, `permute` (`shuffle`; one order for every row, or row keywords `permute(hat: [3, 2, 1, 0])`), `humanize`, `quantize`, `swing`, `chance` (`maybe`), `every`, `ratchet`, besides `loop`/`stretch`/`halftime`/`doubletime`/`legato`/`fill`/`nofill`/`groove`: each runs the Clip method on every row (`lib/mb/sound/sequence/grid.rb`), so a row of the result is the row clip transformed (its own length, loop, alignment: polymetric rows rotate within their lengths), and for rows of one length `kit.T.to_clip` has the notes of `kit.to_clip.T`.  Seeded ones give each row its own seed, `Kit.row_seed(seed, name)` (Dice's hash of the seed and row name, the same in every process), so a humanized kit's rows don't move in lockstep (with one seed, grid rows' equal default seeds 0 drew the same offsets for the nth hit of every row); `seed:` defaults to each row's seed.  `Seq#chance` now multiplies a step's probability like `Clip#chance` (it replaced it, so `grid('..?').chance(0.5)` gave 0.5 as a Seq and 0.25 as a Clip; `Step#chance` still marks one step).  Recipes print the calls (Kit's new public methods are recorded automatically) and graph documents load them.  `swap :drums, beat.reverse` works and a later swap replaces it: `Session::ClipSwaps#kit_swap_mapping` now maps from the nearest row-named clip in a played clip's lineage (the row the player was given), not the earliest (which re-applied every Kit transform the user had swapped in, `loop`, `halftime`, `reverse`, to the next kit); a patch's own kits, the drum machines' shuffle (`Machine.shuffled`), are built `Kit.new(rows, row_names: false)` so a swap still swings the new rows, and an unlooped kit swapped into a looping row is looped (`align:` kept), as the old rederived `loop` did.  Guards: `spec/lib/mb/sound/sequence/transform_parity_spec.rb` (in QuickSelection::ALWAYS: every Kit transform over seeded random kits, per row and stacked, printed and rebuilt, loaded from a document; a parity guard fails a new public Clip method until Kit mirrors it or `kit_exempt` says why, e.g. `transpose` moves a row to another drum voice, `vel` waits for omnibus18), SessionFuzz's kit-swap step (`KIT_TRANSFORMS`, drawn last), `clip_swaps_spec.rb`.
- Note views (parity-1, 2026-10-30; backlog 12.5, for people and agents reading music in the console): `Event#to_s`/`#inspect` print note numbers as Note's names (`A3@0+n8`, `Fs2`, the constants you type; `#<Event A3@0+n8>`) for Integers 12..127 (`Event.note_value?`; lower ones are more often offsets), and `Clip#to_s` decides once per clip, so a clip with any other Integer value (`seq(0, 7, 12)`, cutoffs) prints numbers throughout.  `clip.show(:grid | :roll | :degrees, step:, cycle:, scale:, width:)` / `kit.show(:grid | :roll)` (`Sequence::View`, `sequence/view.rb`; a frozen Data that Pry prints like a Chart, `#to_s` the text, `#rows` the grid strings) draws one cycle in steps found from the notes (the coarsest of 1/16, 1/32, 1/48, 1/64, 1/96 holding every start and end, else 1/32 marked "about"): `:grid` in `grid` characters (x, X, 1-9 velocity ninths, - held, ? chance, f/F flam, | bar lines), one row per note value, or per Kit row, so `grid(16, **beat.show.rows)` rebuilds a kit (the parity sweep checks it over random kits); `:roll` every semitone between the highest and lowest note under a bar ruler; `:degrees` one line per bar of 1-based degrees in `scale:` (the session key's by default): `#4` between scale notes, `'`/`,` octaves above/below the middle note's, `/` chords, `-` held.  Lines wrap at bar lines to 100 columns.  Help: the :sequences topic.
- Generators (decision 5): `Clip#map_cycles` applies list transforms to every generated cycle (`Producer#cycle_mapped`), so `ratchet`, `roll`, `truncate`, `permute` (seeded per cycle; explicit orders raise), and `ghost_chords` (each cycle at its place in the progression, contour from the previous cycle) work per cycle like the `map_clip` transforms; `bake` still bakes N cycles.

## Clips that remember their instrument (play-1, 2026-10-17)

Maintainer, 2026-10-17, after `bg @l_amen` (lesson :chop's chop clip) raised "Cannot play ... Seq in the background": "Unexpected but not sure what the right thing should be"; decided: clips remember the instrument they came from, so `bg :x, chop` / `play chop` equal `chop.sampler(slices)`; plain clips play the soft default patch.

- `Sequence::PlayedBy` (`lib/mb/sound/sequence/played_by.rb`, in Clip and grid Kit): `#instrument` (nil by default; `@instrument` is set to nil in both initializers so every clip has the same ivar shape) and `#played_by(inst)`, a copy that plays on `inst`: anything with `#sampler` (Instrument, Slices, Sample, SF2 font) for clips, a drum machine with `#kit` (`Drums::TR808`, `Drums::TR909`, `kit { }` designs) for clips and grids; nil clears it.  `Sample::Slices#chop` marks its clip with the slices (or `keymap(from:, spread:)` when those aren't the defaults), `Drums::Machine#grid` its grid with the machine.
- Carrying: `PlayedBy.carry!(klass)` (run for Clip, Seq, and Kit in `lib/mb/sound.rb` after everything that adds clip methods loads, before the recipe recorders) prepends a wrapper to every public method except readers, predicates, setters, `to_*`, and `SKIP`: a Clip or Kit result without an instrument, from a receiver with one, comes back as `result.played_by(instrument)` (a copy; nothing is mutated, so a shared clip like `source` never changes).  So `chop.loop.transpose(3).swing(0.2).humanize(...)`, `|`, `&`, `n8`, ... keep it, and new transforms do too without a list.  Methods that return other things (nodes, Notes, Arrays) pass through.
- `Playable.graph` plays a clip on its instrument (`clip.sampler(inst)`, or `inst.kit(clip)` for drum machines) and a grid on `inst.kit(grid)`, else the default patch / `tr808`; spec'd equal sample for sample in a headless Session (`playable_spec.rb`, including the lesson's `@l_amen`).
- Graph documents: chop clips print through their recipe (`brk.chop(...)`), which marks them again on load; `played_by(...)` is a recorded Clip method, printed as written; clips saved as `events(...)` rows don't keep the instrument (an Instrument built region by region already prints as a FIXME).

## Moving parameters in clip operations (midi-consol-2)

`humanize`, `quantize(amount:)`, `swing`, `chance`, `every`, and `transpose` take MIDI::Params (knobs, `cc(...)`, tempo LFOs, clips, arithmetic; see midi.md "Parameters that move"): a param adds a Variation reading at each event's clip position when a cycle's events are made (`every` at the cycle's start), so every cycle reads anew; plain values take the original code (bit-identical).  `Clip::ParamOperations` is prepended to Clip and Seq (Seq's own transpose/chance/every too; a param result is a Clip), passing arguments through as given so recipes print the call as written.  Lengths from params are seconds when plain numbers.  `Clip#value_at(pos)` makes a clip a param itself (`transpose(seq(0, 5, -2, 3).n1.loop)`).  Scale defaults: `scale(:dorian)` without a root takes the session key's root (C until `key` changes it), and `snap`/`degrees`/`lock` without a scale follow the session key at each note; `ghost_chords` without a scale builds its chords in the session key's scale (midi-consol-2b, 2026-10-18).

## Clips on clocks (time-automation, 2026-10-18)

`clip.clocked(x)` (`Sequence::Clocked`, `lib/mb/sound/sequence/clocked.rb`; aliases `clock`, `clocked_at`; by type `clip.at(x)`, `clip.over(x)`, `clip % x` through ArgTypes tables) plays a clip on a clock: its own tempo (`138.75.bpm`), a ratio of the session tempo (`5/4r`), a loop period (`19.7.seconds`: the whole loop spans it), or a named Session clock (`:tide`).  The clock is carried through every transform (`Clocked.carry!`, like PlayedBy), graphs print the canonical `clocked(...)`, and `ClipSource` reads the clip piece by piece from the clock (exact, same at any buffer size).  Details, session time, and documents: arrangement.md "Clocks"; the classifier: arg_types.md.


## Controller lanes (midi-import-1, 2026-10-28)

Clips carried notes only (captures keep just notes and the pedal); now a clip may carry `Sequence::ControlLane`s (`sequence/control_lane.rb`, `sequence/clip_controls.rb`): one controller's values at exact clip positions, `:cc` with a number, `:bend` (-1..1, optional RPN `range:`), `:pressure`, `:poly_pressure` with a note.  `:step` lanes send their points as recorded; curve lanes (nil = monotone cubic through keys with Gesture's Fritsch-Carlson segments, or `:linear`) send a Float value at every multiple of `resolution` (1/512 whole note, 2.6 ms at 180 BPM) between keys.
- `Clip.new(controls:)`, `#controls`, `#control(:cc1 | 1)`, `#with_controls`, `#without_controls`, `#fit_controls(tolerance: 1/127.0, budget:)` (Gesture.fit on each step lane in ticks of the positions' common denominator: the fewest keys of cubic or linear within one 7-bit step; a step that jumps 8 steps or more holds the value before it until just before the jump, `FIT_JUMP`/`FIT_JUMP_TIME`).  `events(..., controls: { cc1: [[pos, value], ...], cc74: { curve: :cubic, points: [...] }, bend: { points:, range: 12 } })` writes lanes; to_ruby and documents print them so.
- Transforms: note transforms (transpose, legato, vel, humanize, quantize, ...) and loop keep lanes (`with_events`/`map_clip`/`map_cycles` pass them); stretch scales them, rotate wraps them (adding the wrap point's value at 0), shift moves them and drops points that leave (a lane moved earlier gets its value at the new start at 0), reverse mirrors step holds, repeat and truncate repeat and cut them, `|` shifts the second clip's, `&` merges by controller.  Generated per-cycle variations don't touch lanes.
- Playback (`MIDI::ClipSource#with_controls`): `Clip#control_edges` gives the values in a read's clip span across loop cycles; controllers go before notes at the same time (a note starts with its step's value).  After a launch, seek, swap, or skipped span (`Source#read` past the position), `Clip#control_chase` sends each lane's value from before the new position (a loop's first cycle has nothing before its start; later cycles see the cycle before), preceded by RPN 0 for bend lanes with a range.  Quiet reads count `Clip#control_horizon` among the next edges, so events are the same at any read size, quiet reads on or off (`clip_source_quiet_spec.rb`, `control_lane_spec.rb`), and the graph sweep plays a synth on stepped, fitted, and bend lanes.

## Rhythm and feel templates (midi-import-1, 2026-10-28)

The maintainer's two meanings of "use its rhythm as a rhythm template", rhythm first (`sequence/rhythm.rb`, `sequence/groove.rb`; help `:templates`):
- (a) `clip.rhythm` (any clip; `Sequence::Rhythm`): hits (start, length, velocity, accented, slid; notes starting together are one hit), length, loop.  `#notes(C1, F1, [A1, E2], nil, Tie, a_clip)` pours pitches in cycling (Arrays chords, nil rests the hit, Tie repeats the pitch before, clips give their notes in order), `#pitches(0, 2, 4, scale:)` scale degrees from 0 (the session key's scale by default), `#to_clip(36)`, `#steps(16)` ('xXx-.' when on the grid).  `rhythm_template([start, length, velocity, { accented:, slid: }], ..., length:, loop:)` builds one (how a rhythm without a recipe prints; named like `groove_template`, since `rhythm` is generative-g3's evolving-rhythm generator).
- (b) `clip.groove` (`Sequence::Groove`): per grid step (16ths, `grid:`, `triplets: true`) over the clip's length, the mean offset of its notes from the step (exact whole notes) and their mean velocity relative to the clip's mean; steps without notes are neutral.  `#to_s` lists each step's offset in percent of a step and its level, so a live take's looseness shows instead of hiding.  `other.groove(template, amount: 1, velocity: amount)` (Clip and Kit; a Clip template is measured first) moves notes onto the template's positions and levels, amount of the way, through `Feel.groove_target` (the quantize step plus the step's offset; loops wrap like quantize).  `Stream#groove` (in `TRANSFORMS`, so Notes and transform specs have it) delays live notes onto the next step's position and scales velocities; it never moves a note earlier.  `groove_template(16, offsets:, levels:)` builds one.
- Rhythms and grooves are recorded objects (`Recipe.graph_object?`), so `take.rhythm.notes(...)` and `beat.groove(take.groove)` print as written.
