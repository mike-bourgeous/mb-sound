# Chorus and tremolo

<!-- summary -->
`sig.chorus(:juno1 | :juno2 | :juno12 | :lush, rate:, depth:, mix:, bbd: false)` (`GraphNode::Chorus`, self-contained in `graph_node/chorus.rb` and `chorus_nodes.rb` for a later effects project) is a Juno-60-style two-tap chorus returning a stereo bundle: mono in like the Juno, clean by default, `bbd: true` adds the board's filters and hiss.  `sig.tremolo(rate, depth:, shape:, phase:, stereo_phase:, seam:, law:, smooth:, mix:)` (aliases `trem`, `amplitude_modulation`; `GraphNode::Tremolo`) is amplitude modulation on the library's LFOs (Hz or tempo-synced; named shapes or a drawn cycle of points), depth in dB down to a floor, `stereo_phase:` for auto-pan; it reproduces Sony Amplitude Modulation's renders to -37..-112 dB.

`sig.chorus(:juno1 | :juno2 | :juno12 | :lush, rate:, depth:, delay:, dry: 1, wet: 1, mix:, bbd: false, cutoff:, hiss:, seed:)` (alias `juno_chorus`; `GraphNode::Chorus`, self-contained in `lib/mb/sound/graph_node/chorus.rb` and `chorus_nodes.rb` so a later effects project can move it) is a Juno-60-style chorus: one MultitapDelay (sinc reads) with two taps swept by triangle Tone LFOs, the right inverted, each added to the dry signal; returns a stereo bundle.  Modes are Pendragon's Juno-60 measurements (I 0.513 Hz, II 0.863 Hz, both 1.66-5.35 ms; I+II 9.75 Hz over 3.3-3.7 ms, alias `:juno_i_ii`); `:lush` (`:juno3`, `:stacked`; not on the hardware) runs I and II at once, four taps, two per side at equal power (`rate:` an Array of two or a number for the first).  Mono in like the Juno: a stereo input is averaged for the delay line while the dry keeps its sides (not run per channel; >2 channels raise).  `rate:` takes Hz, Pitches, Durations (tempo sync), or nodes; `depth:` scales the sweep (number or node); `mix:` (0 dry .. 1 wet, number or node) multiplies `dry:` by 1 - mix and `wet:` by mix.  Clean by default (user's choice); `bbd: true` adds the Juno-60 board's fifth-order lowpasses before and after the delay (all-pole cascades from Holters and Parker, DAFx-18, table 1; wet path -3 dB at 5.42 kHz, `cutoff:` moves it, scaling every pole) and -72 dBFS hiss (`hiss:` a level, `-72.db`, bare numbers linear since db-1; UNVERIFIED: no measured noise floor found; both sides' hiss derive from one seed, `seed:` or one drawn from MB::Sound.seed, the right side seed + 1, since 2026-10-10 so graph-io prints one `seed:`).  When the input ends the chorus plays the delay tail (+50 ms) and ends; the hiss fades out then, or once every Ringdown/Synth upstream ended, so script file renders stop on time.  % of realtime at 128/512-sample buffers: 2.7/2.5 (`bbd` 4.3/3.3, `:lush` 6.0/5.2, `interpolation: :cubic` 1.0/0.7).


## Tremolo (tremolo-1, 2026-11-09)

Amplitude modulation, built for the .acd converter's Sony Amplitude Modulation (it had built one from looping dB tweens, `acd/map/effects/amplitude_mod.rb` on `research-acd-format-linux`) and as a library effect.  The Mac's brief: "Build it on the existing LFO/wavetable shapes and `blend`; don't write a separate oscillator."

- `GraphNode::Tremolo` multiplies its input by a gain that a modulator m (-1..1, clamped, so a band-limited square's ripple never lifts the gain over unity) moves between unity (m = 1) and the trough (m = -1); u = (1 - m) / 2.  `law: :db` (default): gain dB = trough dB x u, the trough stopping at `floor:` (-80 dB); `law: :linear`: gain = 1 - (1 - trough) x u, the classic tremolo, which reaches silence for a full cut.  Stateless (`node_traits memory: :none`), Numo in reused buffers, a plan boundary (no `plan_describe` yet).  Ends with its input.
- `depth:` reads like a ducking amount (`Decibels.reduction_db`, as `duck(amount:)`): -12.db and 12.db are 12 dB down, a bare number is the trough's linear gain (0.25 = 12.04 dB down, 0 = a full cut, above 1 raises), `(-Float::INFINITY).db` a full cut; a node gives the trough's linear gain per sample.
- The LFO is a library Tone: `Tone#lfo` for Hz (a number, Pitch, or node; band-limited shapes fade their band-limiting in above 15 Hz as for any LFO), `Duration#lfo`/`NodeDuration#lfo` for musical lengths (locked to the timeline), in the per-voice LFO's shapes (`Notes::LFO_SHAPES`: `:sine` default, `:triangle`, `:saw`, `:square`, `:random` = a square through `sample_hold`, seeded), with `phase:` (`Tone#with_phase`; every named shape is at its middle rising at phase 0) and `smooth:` (`GraphNode#smooth` on the LFO).  One LFO per distinct channel phase, shared through Tees.
- Drawn shapes: `shape: [[x, y], ...]`, x in 0..1 (rising; two points at one x jump), y from -1 (trough) to 1 (unity), straight lines between points (so straight in dB under `:db`, as Sony draws them), wrapping from the last point to the first.  `TremoloMethods.drawn_table` samples the shape into a 16000-sample cycle `Wavetable` (`mips: false`, `:linear`; a multiple of 1000 so Sony's thousandths and the seam's ends land on samples), kept per shape and seam (64 at most), read by `Tone#wavetable`; a jump takes one table step (1/16000 cycle, 3 samples at 1 Hz).  No band-limiting: at audio rates the gain aliases like Sony's.  `drawn_value`/`seamed_value` are the exact evaluators the table comes from.
- `seam:` (drawn shapes only): a straight line from the shape's value seam/2 before the cycle's end to its value seam/2 after its start, so a jump at the wrap doesn't click.  Sony's "blend graph edges" measured as exactly this with seam 0.1 (`seam: true`): on its 1 Hz ramp render (-24..0 dB) the gain runs -1.2 dB at 0.95 to -22.8 dB at 0.05 in a dB-straight line, the rest of the cycle unchanged.
- `stereo_phase:` (cycles): the right channel's LFO leads (Sony: right(t) = left(t + phase), measured on the 0.2 render); a mono input becomes a stereo bundle when it is nonzero; N channels lead by k/(N-1) of it.  Excluded from per-channel dispatch (`ChannelDispatch::EXCLUDED`) since it builds the per-channel LFOs itself.
- `mix:` goes through `#blend` (linear crossfade); without it no blend nodes are built.  Settings read graph document params at build time (`GraphNode.build_setting`: phase, stereo_phase, seam, points).
- Cost (thread CPU, Linux 7950X under other load, `1.constant` source): 800-sample buffers 17 ns/sample (sine, `:db`), 25 (drawn with a seam), 32 (`:linear`), 36 (depth node); 128-sample buffers 77-110 (a plain `src * 5.hz.lfo` costs 8 / 38).  Same samples at 800, 367, and 1-sample buffers (spec).

### Against Sony Amplitude Modulation

The maintainer's one-knob reference renders of the effect (2026-11-07, 16 tracks; private) through the converter's dry lanes (`AudioRender.build(fx: false)`), the decoded settings mapped as `tremolo(sync ? 1.n4 * period_beats : rate, depth: amplitude.db or 0, shape: points.map { |x, y| [x, 1 + y / 50.0] }, stereo_phase:, seam: blend_edges, law: :db) * wet`, then the converter's one-pole lowpass and dry, nulled against his stems (seconds 12-20 left out: a probe segment there doesn't null even through a constant gain, a dry-lane difference): every track -37 to -112 dB (the converter's tween mapping: -11 to -45 dB, its levels +0.7 dB on blended tracks and -1.7 dB at -inf), levels within 0.03 dB.  Two converter-side findings: amplitude -inf plays as -80 dB (the trough slope on the -inf render, 158 dB per cycle; the converter used -120 dB), and unsynced rates run at an integer period of floor(48000 / rate) samples (the 6.5 Hz preset: -15.7 dB at 6.5 Hz, -42.1 dB at 48000/7384 Hz, -11.5 at 48000/7385); tempo-synced periods at 120 bpm are whole samples already.  The scripts are scratch (not in git); the comparison is reproducible from the converter's decoder and these mappings.
