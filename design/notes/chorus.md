# Chorus

Detailed notes moved from CLAUDE.md (2026-10-10); CLAUDE.md keeps a summary and points here.  Update both when behavior changes.


`sig.chorus(:juno1 | :juno2 | :juno12 | :lush, rate:, depth:, delay:, dry: 1, wet: 1, mix:, bbd: false, cutoff:, hiss:, seed:)` (alias `juno_chorus`; `GraphNode::Chorus`, self-contained in `lib/mb/sound/graph_node/chorus.rb` and `chorus_nodes.rb` so a later effects project can move it) is a Juno-60-style chorus: one MultitapDelay (sinc reads) with two taps swept by triangle Tone LFOs, the right inverted, each added to the dry signal; returns a stereo bundle.  Modes are Pendragon's Juno-60 measurements (I 0.513 Hz, II 0.863 Hz, both 1.66-5.35 ms; I+II 9.75 Hz over 3.3-3.7 ms, alias `:juno_i_ii`); `:lush` (`:juno3`, `:stacked`; not on the hardware) runs I and II at once, four taps, two per side at equal power (`rate:` an Array of two or a number for the first).  Mono in like the Juno: a stereo input is averaged for the delay line while the dry keeps its sides (not run per channel; >2 channels raise).  `rate:` takes Hz, Pitches, Durations (tempo sync), or nodes; `depth:` scales the sweep (number or node); `mix:` (0 dry .. 1 wet, number or node) multiplies `dry:` by 1 - mix and `wet:` by mix.  Clean by default (user's choice); `bbd: true` adds the Juno-60 board's fifth-order lowpasses before and after the delay (all-pole cascades from Holters and Parker, DAFx-18, table 1; wet path -3 dB at 5.42 kHz, `cutoff:` moves it, scaling every pole) and -72 dBFS hiss (UNVERIFIED: no measured noise floor found).  When the input ends the chorus plays the delay tail (+50 ms) and ends; the hiss fades out then, or once every Ringdown/Synth upstream ended, so script file renders stop on time.  % of realtime at 128/512-sample buffers: 2.7/2.5 (`bbd` 4.3/3.3, `:lush` 6.0/5.2, `interpolation: :cubic` 1.0/0.7).
