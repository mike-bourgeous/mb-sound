# Drum machines

Detailed notes moved from CLAUDE.md (2026-10-10); CLAUDE.md keeps a summary and points here.  Update both when behavior changes.


`tr808(source, **knobs)` (`DrumMethods`, `Drums::TR808.kit`; `lib/mb/sound/drums.rb`, `drums/`) is a TR-808-flavored kit, not an exact emulation: a `Drums::Kit` mixing `Drums::Voice`s (kick, snare, rimshot, clap, closed/open hat, cymbal, cowbell, toms, congas, claves, maracas; `kit[:kick]`, `kit.voices`) built from `trigger.ping(freq, decay:)` resonators (`GraphNode::Resonator`, kernel `FastResonator.ping`: a complex one-pole whose frequency moves without changing the ring level), naive-square metal banks (shared by the hats and cymbal), and noise.  Sources: a grid `Sequence::Kit` (rows name voices, aliases, or GM drums), or any MIDI source (clip, `midi`, file, Stream) routed by `TR808::GM_MAP`; knobs per voice as Hashes (`kick: { tune: 45, decay: 1.2 }`; numbers, Lengths, or nodes); the open hat is choked by the closed hat; idle voices are skipped (`skip_idle:`).  Velocity (`Drums.accented`, power curves velocity ** e): `velocity_curve: :grid` (clips, grids, trigger signals) puts x (0.75) `accent:` dB (default 6, user-confirmed) below X; `:midi` (files, live MIDI; chosen from the source by `Drums.velocity_curve`) puts velocity 64 `accent:` dB below 127 (about linear at 6 dB; the grid curve gave -14 dB at 64).  `more_cowbell: true | dB` raises the cowbell's level and its decay × 2^(dB/12).  `outputs: :separate` (alias `:individual`) returns a `GraphNode::Channels` of the voices, named and in `VOICES` order (`outs[:snare]`, `outs.names`; channels may have names in general, kept through per-channel DSL calls), adding up bit for bit to the mix.  Defaults from listening (user, 2026-10-08): snare heads 0.25 s with snappy 0.3 (round 2's louder, longer noise at 0.6 was too noisy), cowbell 0.42 s.  Demo `bin/songs/drums_808.rb`, MIDI script `bin/synths/drums_808.rb`; renders `/app/tmp/listening/drums_808/` (`round2/`).  Not yet: a melodic kick voice, a 909, per-voice pan.
