# Drum machines

<!-- summary -->
`tr808(source, **knobs)` and `tr909(source, **knobs)` (`lib/mb/sound/drums.rb`, `drums/`) are `Drums::Kit`s of resonator, metal-bank, noise, and sample voices, played by a grid `Sequence::Kit` or any MIDI source, with per-voice knob Hashes, accents, chokes, flams, and `outputs: :separate`.  The kit builder (`kit { voice :kick do |h| ... end; choke ... }`, `Drums::Machine`/`Builder`/`Hit`) makes custom kits that tracks, recording, and swaps treat like an 808.  Idle voices sleep on the 800-sample `Synth::SKIP_GRID`, wake on the exact hit, end on exact frames, and catch their noise up, so output is the same at any buffer size (delay-sleep-1).  Voice settings bind as live knobs (`bind :drums, tr909: :kick_decay`; kit-live-1).  Kits borrow shipped designs: `borrow :fit909_kick`, the fitted 909 kick (`L.kit.fit909_kick`).
<!-- /summary -->

## Quick reference

- `tr808(grid(16, bd: 'x...', snare: '..x.'), bd: { decay: 0.8 })`, `tr909(...)`, `outputs: :separate`; custom kits: `kit(source) { voice(:kick) { |h| h.body(50) + h.click }; tr808 :open_hat; choke :open_hat, :closed_hat }`.
- Claps: `h.env(0.02, curve: 30, trigger: h.bursts(4, 11.ms, falloff: 0.9)) + h.env(0.5, trigger: h.repeats([[22.ms, 1.0]])) * 0.2` (`Hit#env` `trigger:`, `Hit#repeats`; the same as `Drums.decay_env(Drums::Repeats.new(h.t, ...), ...)`).
- Code `lib/mb/sound/drums.rb`, `lib/mb/sound/drums/` (`Kit`, `Voice` with its `Tap`s, `Machine`, `Builder`); spec `spec/lib/mb/sound/drums/kit_spec.rb`; demo `bin/songs/kit_builder.rb`; help `help :drums`.
- Idle voices sleep on the 800-sample `Synth::SKIP_GRID` and catch their noise up, so output is the same at any buffer size.
- Fitted voices: `L.kit.fit909_kick.(grid(16, kick: 'X...X...X...X...').loop)`, `kit(beat) { borrow :fit909_kick; tr909 except: :kick }`; ranged knobs `voice :kick, decay: (0.1..2.0).at(0.47)`; code `lib/mb/sound/drums/fits.rb`, spec `spec/lib/mb/sound/drums/fits_spec.rb`.

Detailed notes moved from CLAUDE.md (2026-10-10); the summary block above feeds the generated index (`design/notes/README.md`, `rake docs:index`).  Update it when behavior changes.


`tr808(source, **knobs)` (`DrumMethods`, `Drums::TR808.kit`; `lib/mb/sound/drums.rb`, `drums/`) is a TR-808-flavored kit, not an exact emulation: a `Drums::Kit` mixing `Drums::Voice`s (kick, snare, rimshot, clap, closed/open hat, cymbal, cowbell, toms, congas, claves, maracas; `kit[:kick]`, `kit.voices`) built from `trigger.ping(freq, decay:)` resonators (`GraphNode::Resonator`, kernel `FastResonator.ping`: a complex one-pole whose frequency moves without changing the ring level), naive-square metal banks (shared by the hats and cymbal), and noise.  Sources: a grid `Sequence::Kit` (rows name voices, aliases, or GM drums), or any MIDI source (clip, `midi`, file, Stream) routed by `TR808::GM_MAP`; knobs per voice as Hashes (`kick: { tune: 45, decay: 1.2 }`; numbers, Lengths, or nodes); the open hat is choked by the closed hat; idle voices are skipped (`skip_idle:`; same samples at any buffer size since 2026-10-13: a voice falls asleep only on the `Synth::SKIP_GRID` 800-sample grid after `SLEEP_TIME` of silent grid cells and wakes on the exact hit sample, splitting buffers as needed; before, both moved with buffer edges, so metal voices and noise differed at 367/368-sample buffers) (guard: `spec/lib/mb/sound/buffer_size_invariance_synth_spec.rb`).  What a sleeping voice still reads (`Voice#boundary`: its branches of Tees read elsewhere, e.g. the shared metal bank, and its Notes nodes) is decided when it skips, cached on `Tee.branch_changes` (fixes-9, 2026-10-13: it was fixed at the first buffer, so a reader made later fell behind the voice and waking raised `BranchBufferOverflow`); branches made while building the voice's graph and read by nothing in it (`TR808.voice` wraps the build in `Tee.collect_branches` and passes `built:`) don't count, like Synth lanes since fixes-8.  The metal bank's naive squares at 540 and 800 Hz have jumps exactly on samples, which used to land a sample apart by block size; naive shapes now snap onto jumps within 1e-9 cycles (see oscillators).  A grid kit with an open hat and no closed hat used to raise (the grid's source Hash created empty entries; fixed 2026-10-13).  A `Resonator` whose input has ended now stops on the exact sample where its ring falls below -120 dB (binary search over kernel runs in the last buffer; it used to end at the first buffer start below -120 dB, so a sum of pings, which ends with its first input, cut off at buffer-dependent times: the zen persona's gong, 2026-10-13).  A voice played by a finite source ends on an exact frame of its own time (fix-808-fuzz, 2026-11-01): `QUIET_TIME` after the later of the frame where its last trigger input ended (a Notes trigger ends on its own 800-sample grid after the clip's end) and the sample after its last one at -90 dB or louder, with a short last buffer, and the `Kit` ends with its last voice inside the buffer (they ended at the first whole buffer after `QUIET_TIME` of quiet buffers, so a render of a one-shot grid was 25600 frames at 800-frame buffers and 26424 at 367 once session-2's exact render ends made the length count: SessionFuzz seed 42 of ux-notes-2; QuietRun's `:buffer` mode, its last user, is gone).  Sources whose triggers never end while their Notes have (a stream with no known end) still count from the buffer where the Notes were seen ended.  The invariance helper (`expect_invariant`) now compares where nodes end, and sweeps one-shot 808 and 909 kits.  A trigger source that ends inside a buffer (a one-shot clip launched off the 800-sample grid) gives a short last buffer; `Voice#sample` pads it with zeros so the voice rings on (fixes-14, 2026-10-15: full renders of drums_808/drums_909 crashed at bar 13's one-step crash clip, "trigger has 685 samples, not 800"; the smoke tests' half-bar renders never reach it; `kit_spec.rb`).  Randomness (noise): `seed:` (default drawn from MB::Sound.seed); voice i in `VOICES` order is built in `MB::Sound.with_seed(seed + 1 + i)` (2026-10-10, so graph-io prints one `seed:`; noise values changed from before, no A/B needed per the maintainer).  Velocity (`Drums.accented`, power curves velocity ** e): `velocity_curve: :grid` (clips, grids, trigger signals) puts x (0.75) `accent:` (a gain, default `6.db`, user-confirmed; bare numbers are linear gains since db-1, 2026-10-14) below X; `:midi` (files, live MIDI, performed clips; chosen from the source by `Drums.velocity_curve`) puts velocity 64 `accent:` dB below 127 (about linear at 6 dB; the grid curve gave -14 dB at 64).  Recording (fixes-5, 2026-10-13): clips recorded from live input are `Clip#performed?`, so a recorded drum loop plays with the monitor's :midi curve (it came back up to 6 dB softer through :grid), and a kit built for a performed clip (the recording's empty placeholder) has every voice of the map; `Kit#plays_clip?` tells Arrangement tracks to build the patch again rather than swap in a clip the kit wasn't built for (other voices or another curve).  Armed tr808 tracks monitor live input (`LiveClip#to_midi_stream`).  `more_cowbell: true | 6.db` raises the cowbell's level and its decay × 2^(dB/12).  `outputs: :separate` (alias `:individual`) returns a `GraphNode::Channels` of the voices, named and in `VOICES` order (`outs[:snare]`, `outs.names`; channels may have names in general, kept through per-channel DSL calls), adding up bit for bit to the mix.  Defaults from listening (user, 2026-10-08): snare heads 0.25 s with snappy 0.3 (round 2's louder, longer noise at 0.6 was too noisy), cowbell 0.42 s.  Demo `bin/songs/drums_808.rb`, MIDI script `bin/synths/drums_808.rb`; renders `/app/tmp/listening/drums_808/` (`round2/`).  Not yet: a melodic kick voice, per-voice pan (the 909 came in drums-2, a7090b95, below; `kit` voices can play melodies through a tune knob, as kit_builder's log drum does).

## Kit builder and TR-909 (drums-2, 2026-10-13)

Stage 2 of "Sampler + second drum machine" (maintainer: "Modular and 909").  Files: `lib/mb/sound/drums/machine.rb` (Drums::Machine, Builder), `hit.rb` (Drums::Hit), `nodes.rb` (Repeats, ChokeGate), `tr909.rb`; `DrumMethods#kit` (alias `drum_kit`) and `#tr909` (`tr909(source, **knobs)`, a Machine: analog kick/snare/toms/rim/4-burst clap, hats/crash/ride as 6-bit 32 kHz ROM Samples synthesized at first use, `TR909.rom`).  A design in one line: `kit { voice :kick, tune: 50 do |h| h.body(h.tune, sweep: 3) + h.click end; choke :open_hat, by: :closed_hat }`; kit blocks borrow voices by name (`tr909 :clap`, `tr808 :cowbell`).

- **Designs are values.** `kit { ... }` makes a `Drums::Machine` (voices in definition order, chokes, aliases, GM notes); `kit(source) { ... }` / `machine.kit(source, **opts)` / `machine.(source)` build a `Drums::Kit` of `Drums::Voice`s exactly as tr808 does (same Voice, Tap, idle skipping, `outputs: :separate`, `plays_clip?` notes and curve), so arrangement tracks, recording, and swaps treat a custom kit like an 808.  TR909 is a Machine defined with the same builder (dogfooding); `TR808.machine` wraps `TR808::Build` for borrowing (`tr808 :cowbell` in a kit block; tr808 itself is unchanged: shared metal banks, more_cowbell, its own seeds).
- **Voice blocks** get a `Drums::Hit`: the accented trigger (`t`), one reader per knob (defined as singleton methods, so typos are NoMethodErrors with did_you_mean; knob names that are Hit methods are refused), and layers played by the trigger at the hit's velocity: `body`/`ping` (resonator with `sweep:` and `drop:` pitch envelopes), `noise` (filters + decay env), `click`, `metal` (808 square bank), `sample` (a Sample through SamplerVoice; tune in semitones; a node goes in as the sampler's per-hit `shift:` since kit-live-1, before as `freq:` relative to the root's equal-tempered Hz), `fm` (two operators reset by the hit, `index_decay:`), `bursts` (Repeats), `repeats` (Repeats of the hit at given `[[delay, gain], ...]`; fit-patch-2), `env` (`trigger:` plays it from another trigger, such as the bursts or a repeat; fit-patch-2), `sweep`, `velocity` (sample-and-hold of the trigger).  A block without parameters runs on the Hit (instance_exec), with one gets it as `h`; the kit block likewise for the Builder.  Every voice has a `level` knob applied after its block, and takes `accent:`, `velocity_curve:`, `flam:` in its knob Hash.  `voice :name, sample` makes a sample voice (knobs tune, decay).  Kits are mono: a voice whose graph has several channels (a stereo Sample through `h.sample` or `voice :name, sample`) plays them mixed down (`Channels#mono`, the average; omnibus17, Kez's K5 and Odile's F12: it raised "undefined method 'get_sampler' for Channels" in `Drums::Voice#initialize`); pan or widen the kit after it.
- **Routing**: grid rows by voice name, alias (`as:`), or GM drum name; MIDI by `gm:` notes (default: the GM note of the voice's name; custom names have none unless given, and `machine.grid(16, zap: '...')` builds grids naming custom voices, since `grid` itself needs GM names or `map:`).  `hh:` is now a grid alias of the closed hat.
- **Chokes**: `choke :a, :b, :c` (each cuts off the others) or `choke :open_hat, by: :closed_hat`.  Implemented as a `ChokeGate` multiplying the voice's output (3 ms linear ramp like Envelope's choke, back to 1 at the voice's own hit; a hit beats a choke on the same sample), so any layer (rings, samples) chokes; the choker's notes are read by the voice's second input as in tr808.
- **Shuffle and flams**: `shuffle:` 0..6 delays every second 16th by n/48 beat (2n ticks of 96 PPQN; 4 is the triplet feel 2/3), or a Float swing amount, through `Clip#swing` on grid rows and clips (other sources raise; a grid's swung rows are a `Kit.new(..., row_names: false)`, so `swap :drums, kit2` swings kit2's rows again, parity-1).  Flams are a note mark (round 2, maintainer's choice; round 1's `flams:` second source is gone, no alias): `D2.flam`, `D2.flam(25.ms)`, `step.flam`, grid characters 'f' (flam) and 'F' (accented flam).  The mark is a field of `Seq::Step`, `Sequence::Event` (`events(..., { flam: true | seconds })`, `to_s` ' flam'/' flam20ms'), and `MIDI::Event#flam` (ClipSource passes it like pitch marks); `Notes#flam_mark` holds it per note-on (0, seconds, or -1 for true).  As MIDI 1.0 bytes a flam-marked note-on is the plain note-on: MIDI has no per-note flam, so outside gear hears one hit and .mid files don't keep flams.  Kits (Machine and tr808) wrap a voice's trigger in `Drums::Flams` when the source has any flam mark (`Drums.flams?`, static over clips, grids, and Notes/Streams reading them; live input and files never): the hit's sample gets a grace stroke (GRACE 0.6 of the velocity, before the accent curve, so -12.6 dB at the default 6 dB accent), and the hit lands the mark's time (or the kit's `flam:`, 16 ms (round 2; 18 before), or a voice's `flam:` knob) later, on exact samples at any buffer size.  Flams sits in the voice's first input, so a sleeping voice wakes on the second stroke; it alternates two output buffers (a Tap's Tee branches share them).  `Kit#plays_clip?` refuses a clip with flams for a kit built without them, so arrangement swaps rebuild the patch.  Other voices ignore flam marks.  Note: NoteNodes of one Notes must be read trigger first (reading `flam_mark` before `trigger` in a test shifted the trigger by a buffer; Flams reads the trigger first).
- **Idle skipping**: a voice also stays awake while any SamplerVoice or Repeats in its graph isn't idle (a reversed sample's quiet start, a burst more than SLEEP_TIME after the hit used to be skipped; found with the kit-builder demo's reversed swell).  Output is the same at any buffer size (specs at 7/64/367/800/2048).
- **TR-909 voices**: kick = ping with a fast sweep (punch × 5 over 18 ms) and a slower 25% drop, driven into a softclip, plus a click and a 6 ms noise tick (attack); snare = two swept heads (f, 1.62 f) and noise (snappy level and length, tone opens the lowpass); clap = four bursts 8.5 ms apart (Repeats, falloff 0.85) and a tail; toms = swept pings with a droop and a noise breath; rimshot = three pings highpassed and clipped.  Hats, crash, ride: `TR909.rom(name)`, synthesized at first use (0.15 s for all four, cached per process with a Mutex): the product of two square banks (METAL_A × METAL_B, ring modulation for density) mixed with seeded noise (ROM_SEED, so the same "ROM" every time), double highpassed, a fast + slow decay, bell partials for the ride, then `Sample::Lofi` hold at 32 kHz and 6 bits of full scale; played by the sampler with decay envelopes and tune (semitones).  No recorded cymbals anywhere.
- **Graph documents**: `tr909(...)` prints as its call.  Kit blocks print as their source (runtime blocks to the recorder, so locals from outside print FIXMEs, as for other runtime blocks), and load without trust: the loader treats `kit`, `drum_kit`, and `voice` as build blocks; a build proc run with instance_exec gives its Scope an implicit self (`Scope#implicit_self`), where receiverless calls go first; Registry::VALUES allows Builder (voice, choke, tr808, tr909) and Hit methods, plus each hit's knob names; `bursts` counts against the copies limit.
- **Plan layer**: voices stay unplanned like tr808's (Voice reads Taps itself; SamplerVoice is a plan boundary).  Not attempted.
- **Taps copy** (cleanup-2, 2026-10-31): `Voice::Tap` gives the voice graph its own copy of each unfrozen source buffer, made when the graph samples it, since the Voice samples its sources (rewriting their reused buffers) before the graph's Tees reach their next frame; frozen buffers (Notes steady constants, the Voice's zeros) pass as they are, so steady-input caches stay steady.  Found by the full-Ruby graph sweep (kernels.md); the sweep's Notes reference pass covers it.
- **Measured** (AMD 7950X, Ruby without YJIT; an 11-voice 909 grid kit, every voice playing, table builds included): 2 s rendered in 0.22 s at 128-sample buffers (11% of realtime) and 0.05 s at 800 (2.7%).
- Demos: `bin/songs/drums_909.rb` (house into techno, shuffle and a flammed roll), `bin/synths/drums_909.rb` (MIDI, CC 16-19), `bin/songs/kit_builder.rb` (the junkyard kit: scratch kick, FM can, a log drum whose tune knob plays a melody, a reversed bounced swell, borrowed 909 clap and 808 cowbell); renders in `/app/tmp/listening/drums-2/`.
- **Round 2 (maintainer's Bench picks, 2026-10-13)**: kick defaults punch 0.9, drive 0.8, attack 0.8 (was 0.6, 0.4, 0.5); hats tune -3 semitones and short (closed decay 0.06, open 0.3; was 0 and 0.16 / 0.9); flam spacing 16 ms (DEFAULT_FLAM, was 18); the snare unchanged; toms reworked after the 808's were preferred: tunes 100/145/200 Hz, decays 1.1/0.95/0.8 s (was 95/135/190, 0.55/0.5/0.45), a gentle strike (sweep 0.3 over 20 ms, was 0.9 over 10 ms) and a long 35% glide over 0.35 s, a soft clip for roundness, a darker noise breath (bandpass 3x the pitch, was 5x).  The machine default stays straight (shuffle nil); the house template plays shuffle 2.  New demo `bin/songs/even_more_cowbell.rb` (`MB::Sound.cowbell_kit`: the scratch kick and snare, the 808 cowbell tuned by a clip, an FM bell, a bounced 808 cowbell crushed with `lofi(:sp1200)` played an octave down, and `fever`, one knob for level and ring).  The 909 cymbals render bit-identically after sampler-2 (b766c403: per-sample reads; the hits pass `interpolation: :optimal` and the ROM samples keep the default read).
- **Round 3 (drums-3, 2026-10-13, the maintainer's round 2 verdicts)**: the 909 toms are the round 1 voices again (95/135/190 Hz, decays 0.55/0.5/0.45 s, sweep 0.9 over 10 ms, 40% drop over 0.25 s, noise at 5x the pitch, level 0.75: "the round 1 toms are fine, I just like the 808 toms a lot"; renders within -69 dB of the round 1 Bench file).  **Flams play the grace early** for sources whose notes are known ahead ("The flams at the end make the hit sound out of time"): `MIDI::ClipSource#play_flams_early(spacing, sample_rate:)` sends flam-marked note-ons early by the mark's own time or the kit's spacing, rounded to whole samples, so `Drums::Flams` (unchanged: grace on the note-on, main hit the spacing later) puts the main hit on the exact sample of the step.  The source reads the clip ahead by the longest spacing (never past a pending swap; read-ahead flams for steps after a swap or jump are dropped) and returns the queued note-ons in time order, so output is the same at any buffer size.  Kits turn it on for grid rows (`Drums.hits(notes, flam, early: true)`, each row its own ClipSource with the voice's spacing) and for a Clip's shared stream when every voice uses the kit's spacing (`voice_sources(early_flam:)`, Machine and TR808).  Live MIDI, a Notes or Stream passed in, and clips with per-voice spacings keep the delayed main hit (the grace on the step), as does a flam within its spacing of a launch, jump, or swap, where there was nothing to read ahead of.  `even_more_cowbell` is all cowbells now ("I wanted *all* the rows to be based on cowbells"): the kick is the 808 cowbell bounced to a sample, two octaves down and lowpassed at 700 Hz, over a swept sub; the snare the same sample a fourth down with the noise burst; closed and open hats the cowbell's 540/800 Hz partials five times higher, highpassed at 6.5 kHz, the open hat choked by the closed.  Renders `/app/tmp/listening/drums-3/`.
- **Voice settings are params (editability-1, 2026-11-01; Note 15, the maintainer's "every parameter should be editable in the sound design inspector", its first piece of "library code takes params")**: `Drums::Voice#param_info` is the voice's settings (its knobs but the kit-wide accent, velocity curve, and flam), so each voice is an inspector row of its own (SOURCE, what "voice"; before, hidden plumbing with a dim line of values under the voice strip), and `bind :drums, tr909: :kick_decay` (or Enter, Left/Right on the row's decay) makes the track knob `:"drums/kick_decay"` (`LiteralBinding.kit_target`: a member of the kit call's Hash for the voice, `kick: { decay: ... }`, written or not, under the key the code used, `bd:` included; ranges from `LiteralBinding.kit_range`: amounts 0..1, levels 0..2, tunes in Hz or the 909 samples' semitones).  These were build knobs (the knob's number given while the kit built; each turn rebuilt the kit, no held sweeps) until kit-live-1 made them live (below).  Same for tr808, dnb_drums and the other presets playing a kit, and Machine kits (`kit(c) { voice ... }`).  The numbers inside a voice's code (an 808 hat's bandpass, an envelope's sustain) say `set inside the tr808 closed hat's code; its settings are the closed_hat row's (tune, decay, tone, level)`.
- **Live kit knobs (kit-live-1, 2026-11-02; the maintainer's "Yes, live kit knobs")**: `LiteralBinding.kit_target` binds a voice setting `:live`: the kit call's Hash member gets `knob(:"drums/kick_decay")` and the voices read it as a node (they took nodes for every setting already), so `turn :"drums/kick_decay", 1.5, over: 2.bars` sweeps the playing kit without a rebuild, MIDI controllers and follows move it, and a saved set prints the knob.  A voice reading a setting once while building would fall back to a build knob (`Arrangement#bind_probe`); the editability guard fails for any shipped kit's setting that does (tr909, tr808, dnb_drums, funk_beat, funk_perc, jungle_perc, a kit builder kit).  `Hit#sample` takes a node tune as the sampler's per-hit `shift:` (semitones, summed like `transpose:` in the kernel), not `freq:` (Hz relative to the key's frequency: a float32 ratio that moved the 909's crash by -74 dBFS over its ring), so a knob tune retunes the next hit, not a ringing one (an LFO on a sample voice's tune now steps per hit too).  A sleeping voice advances its knob readers past the frames it slept (`Drums::Voice#boundary`, as skipped Synth lanes do), so a voice waking inside a buffer reads the knob's value on that frame (they gave the buffer's first frames: a ramp of the 808 hat's tone landed -66 dB apart at 367- vs 800-frame buffers, its metal tune 0.23; `buffer_size_invariance_synth_spec.rb`).  Unbound kits are unchanged, bit for bit.  Null residuals with every numeric setting a Knob at its default (4 s of a busy grid, worst peak difference per voice): tr909 -138.5 dBFS (snare; kick, rimshot -144.5; the rest bit for bit); tr808 -135.0 (snare; the rest -137.5 or less, most bit for bit), except the hats and cymbal with a knob tune: a knob tune can't share the kit's metal bank (only equal numbers do), and a voice's own bank sleeps with it, so its phases differ from the shared bank's (-10.5 dBFS peak, 0 dB rms: the same sound, not a null; a fix would need the bank to keep time while asleep).  CPU (Mac container, YJIT off, plans on, every voice playing; % of realtime, numbers -> every setting a knob, the worst case): tr909 14.3 -> 16.4 at 128 frames, 6.1 -> 6.7 at 512; tr808 13.1 -> 16.5, 5.2 -> 6.3 (three metal banks); the knob arithmetic fuses (909: 16 regions over 168 -> 239 nodes; 808: 18 -> 17 over 210 -> 430).  Bench set `/app/tmp/listening/kit-live-1/`.
- **Silent voices (duck-keys, 2026-10-18)**: `voice :trigger, silent: true` (Builder#voice; Machine#silent_names, Kit#silent; `VoiceSpec#silent`, kept when borrowed) plays like any voice, so a sidechain reads it (`duck(:drums, only: :trigger)` through the track's spy, exact at any buffer size), but `Drums::Kit` never adds it to the mix and `outputs: :separate` leaves it out (it raises if only silent voices play).  Without a block it plays `Machine::TRIGGER_PULSE`: `h.env(0.03, attack: 0, curve: 0)`, the hit's velocity falling linearly to 0 over 30 ms (a one-sample impulse barely moves a 1 ms duck attack); with a block anything (a ghost kick body).  A kit with silent voices mixes bit for bit like the kit without them (same seed).  Silent voices have no GM note unless given `gm:`; grids name them (`m.grid(16, trigger: 'x...')`).  tr808 itself has no silent voices (`kit { tr808; voice :trigger, silent: true }` builds the 808 voices through the Machine, each with its own metal bank).
- **Params in clap settings (fit-patch-2, 2026-11-04; the hand-tune queue's 909 clap fit)**: `Hit#bursts` reads its spacing (through `Length.seconds`) and falloff, and `Drums::Repeats` each delay and gain, as numbers while the voice builds when given a param or knob (`GraphNode.build_setting`), so a fit document's params load and a track's knobs are build knobs (a turn rebuilds the kit); the burst count stays a number (copies, counted against a document's limits).  `Hit#env(decay, trigger:)` and `Hit#repeats(taps, trigger:)` write the fitted clap without the module helpers (bit for bit the same as `Drums.decay_env` on `Drums::Repeats.new`, machine_spec), and documents may call `Drums.decay_env`, `Drums.mix`, and `Drums.hz` for code printed with them.  Numbers render unchanged.

## Fitted voices: the 909 kick (kick-lib-1, 2026-11-04)

The maintainer on the round-4 Bench item for the 909 kick fitted to a recorded house beat: "I like the round 3 kick -- save it to the library.  But none of them sound like the reference.  I'll hand-tune for the analyzer's sake."  The kick is research-voice-match-3's round 3 structure fit (`research/voice-match/fit909_r3.rb`, experiment `kick_thejames_l3`: research-909-sound's structure kick, the tr909 kick's own voice with its constants freed, fitted by CMA-ES under the round 3 L3 ear loss).  **Measurement source**: "TR-909 House Beat.wav" by Thejames428 on Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0, measured only and not distributed; only the fitted numbers ship (the 909 study's licensing approach, research-909-sound README).

- **Form**: a one-voice `Drums::Machine` (`Drums::Fits.fit909_kick`, `lib/mb/sound/drums/fits.rb`; `Fits::DESIGNS`) registered as the kit entry `L.kit.fit909_kick` (family :machine, tags 909 kick house techno).  Chosen over a graph document (presets/, the fit patch form) and a tr909 knob preset because one design then plays on its own (`L.kit.fit909_kick.(grid(16, kick: 'X...X...X...X...').loop)`, `track :kick, clip, :fit909_kick`, `play L.kit.fit909_kick`) and lends its voice to any kit (`kit(beat) { borrow :fit909_kick; tr909 except: :kick }`, the 909 with this kick); a document can't be borrowed into a kit, and a tr909 preset would have needed ten more knobs on the tr909's kick (its sweep and drop times, clip threshold, click tone, tick filter) to express the fit.  The entry builds through `kit(source, name: :fit909_kick) { borrow :fit909_kick }`, so graphs record, save, and load it as written (graph documents allow `borrow`).
- **Knobs** (ranged: the fit's search ranges): tune 51.328155 Hz (30..110), decay 0.468972 s (0.1..2), punch `2.061796 / 5` (the pitch sweep / 5, as on the tr909's kick; 0..2), drop 0.283811 (the slow drop; 0..0.8), drive `Math.log2(2.460364) / 2` (the gain is 2^(2 drive), as on the tr909's; 0..1.5), attack `0.732949 / 0.9` (the click is attack × 0.9; 0..1.5), tick 0.562526 (the noise tick; 0..1.5), plus level.  The written expressions give the fitted numbers back exactly through those formulas (checked in fits_spec), so the shared knob names mean what they mean on the tr909's kick and the entry renders the Bench's console code **bit for bit** (as an entry, borrowed, as the design, after a document round trip, and with a live knob bound to drop at its value).  The other fitted numbers stay in the voice's code: sweep time 80 ms, drop time 37.7 ms, clip threshold 0.205, click tone 0.091, tick decay 36 ms, highpass 482 Hz, lowpass 4.88 kHz.
- **Ranged voice knobs** (any Machine): `voice :kick, decay: (0.1..2.0).at(0.47)` reads the default as before and keeps a Param (`VoiceSpec#params`, `Drums::Voice#knob_params`; borrowed voices keep them); `bind` makes the knob with that range (`LiteralBinding::Target#range`, read first by `kit_range`), and the library entry's params are the voice's knobs named as bind names them (`kick_decay`).
- **Borrowing**: `borrow design, *voices, except:, **knobs` takes a shipped design's name (`Drums.designs`: tr909, tr808, the fits; did-you-mean on others), a library kit entry, or a Machine; `tr909`/`tr808` in kit blocks take `except:` too.  A name the kit already has raises, naming the except: that leaves one out.  Borrow the kick first to keep every 909 voice on its tr909 seed (voice i is built with seed + 1 + i): the other voices are then bit for bit the tr909's.
- **Auditions and Bench beats** play only the rows a kit entry lists (`settings[:voices]`, `Library::Audition.kit_rows`): the kick row of the demo beat.  Pins fingerprint a fitted design by its method source, so a refit warns songs that pinned it.
- Not done: track knobs made from the entry's params on `track :kick, clip, :fit909_kick` (kit entries get knobs by `bind` or the inspector, as tr909 does); a graph-document twin for the tuner's retune and refine (the tuner lists kit entries by ear only).
