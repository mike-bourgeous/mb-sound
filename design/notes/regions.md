# Timeline regions

<!-- summary -->
`regions([sample, at, length, { offset:, gain:, transpose:, fade_in:, fade_out:, curve_in:, curve_out: }], ...)` (`SamplerMethods#regions`) is a lane of audio regions on the timeline: `MB::Sound::Region` values played by `GraphNode::RegionPlayer` (a TimelineNode and plan boundary; a Channels bundle for stereo), the placement fields of a DAW's audio events (ACID's, from the .acd study).  Overlaps add (crossfades), fades follow ACID's renderer in its six curves (`:linear :fast :slow :smooth :sharp :hold`), and a seek or launch inside a region plays it from the source sample an uninterrupted run is on, the same at any buffer size.  Prints and saves as `regions(...)` rows.  Library building block for the timeline options (A-E) the maintainer hasn't chosen between yet.
<!-- /summary -->

## Quick reference

- `lane = regions([vox, 0r, 2r, { offset: 1.5, fade_out: 1/4r, curve_out: :slow }], [vox, 7/4r, 2r, { fade_in: 1/4r }])`; `bg :vox, lane`, `track :vox, lane`; `align: :launch`, `channels:`.
- Rows: positions and lengths in whole notes (Integers, Rationals; 1 is a 4/4 bar), Durations, or fixed Lengths (converted at the tempo where the player starts); `nil` length plays to the source's end; `offset:` source seconds or a Length; `gain:` linear or dB; `transpose:` semitones (varispeed).
- Code `lib/mb/sound/region.rb`, `lib/mb/sound/graph_node/region_player.rb`, `SamplerMethods#regions`; spec `spec/lib/mb/sound/region_player_spec.rb`; graph sweep factory "region lanes (RegionPlayer)"; SessionFuzz's region command; help `help :sampler`.
- Related: one-shot sampler voices resuming at seeks (`sampler.md`, "One-shots across seeks"), ACID's curve names (`curves.md`).

Branch `regions-1` (2026-11-04).  The maintainer (2026-11-04): "Go ahead with some of the supporting library work while Linux works on the file format and its proof of concept audio renderer."  Background: the Mac session's 2026-10-31 answer on how far mb-sound is from a DAW timeline (`/app/tmp/acd-work/timeline-writeup.md`: a placement list per track with a `PlacementPlayer` node) and the Linux session's draft `research/acd-format/TIMELINE.md` on `research-acd-format-linux` (options A-E), whose `acd/audio_render.rb` renders ACID audio events offline and nulls against ACID's own renders (-12..-15 dB).  That renderer's placement is the reference here (read only; no ACID files or the maintainer's audio in specs).

## Decisions

- **A node, not a clip kind** (the maintainer's open TIMELINE.md Q2 stays open): `regions(...)` builds a graph node, so a track plays it today as its graph (`track :vox, lane`) or a `bg` player does, without deciding whether a region is a new clip kind or a `Sample` clip grown offset/length/fade options.  Either answer can wrap it: a region clip kind would build a RegionPlayer from its rows; a Sample clip's options map onto one Region.  A first-class timeline (option C) would give each track a lane of these; option A's "one region clip per track over the whole song" is a lane in one scene; B/D cut lanes at section boundaries (a region split at a boundary is two rows, the second with its offset advanced: offsets make that exact).
- **Rows like `events(...)`**: `[sample, at, length, { options }]`, whole notes as exact Rationals (Floats raise, as in events rows), options only where they differ from the defaults, so a lane prints (`to_ruby`) and saves (graph documents, bundles: samples print through their lineage or as bundle assets) as the call that made it, and loads to the same samples (spec).  `regions` is in SamplerMethods (a recorded top-level module); it reads no files itself (samples come from `Sample[...]`, a FILE_METHODS reader).
- **Positions follow the transport, audio plays at its own rate**: a region starts on the first frame at or after its timeline position (Rational whole notes against the transport's whole notes per frame, the same Rational arithmetic as LoopPlayer), and once started it counts frames, so its length in frames is fixed at the tempo where it started (a tempo change mid-region doesn't stretch or cut it differently; its next start follows the new tempo).  Length/fade values given as seconds convert at the tempo where the player starts (like `:time` markers).
- **Exactness at any buffer size**: each playing region keeps an Integer frame count `k` since its first frame and the Rational fraction `f` (0...1) of a frame its start fell before that frame; every frame's source position and fade position is computed from `(k + j)` (an exact integer double) and constants, so the same frame gives the same floats whatever block holds it (spec: 800 vs 367/368, and 64/1031 under SLOW=1, bit for bit, including a transposed region off the frame grid).  A region whose position falls between one block's last frame and the next block's first starts on the next block's frame 0 (until fix-bufsize-1107 neither block started it: SessionFuzz seed 532169, a regions lane under a tempo change, silent from a region's start at 800 frames).  Seeks (`timeline_start`) compute `e = (time - at) / step` for regions covering the target and split it the same way, so a seek of a constant-tempo lane lands on the uninterrupted run's samples exactly (spec: bit for bit at 800 and 367; `render(from:, preroll: false)` within 1e-6 of the pre-roll render through FLAC).
- **Reads**: speed-1 regions of samples at the output rate read whole source samples (the region's first source sample on its first frame; offsets rounded to whole samples, as ACID's renderer rounds), so they are copies times a gain, no interpolation filter; other speeds (`transpose:`, other source rates) read Catmull-Rom interpolated positions with zeros past the ends (as LoopPlayer's `:resample`; no band-limiting, so transposing up aliases a little: the sampler's band-limited tables would be the upgrade).  Reads past the source's end are silent and end the region.
- **Fades as ACID's renderer**: frame e of a fade-in of n frames gains `curve((e + 0.5) / n)`, a fade-out's frame `1 - curve(x)` the same way counted toward the region's end (or the source's end for `nil` lengths); fades and region gain multiply; overlapping regions add, so an overlap with matching fades is a crossfade (ACID's crossfade flag picks the fade-out's curve: an importer sets `curve_out:`).  ACID's fade code 0 ("no fade") is no fade, not `:hold` (`curves.md`).
- **align**: `:timeline` (default) places regions from bar 1, as a DAW lane is placed; `:launch` from the graph's launch (a lane used like a clip launched at a bar).  A fresh launch inside a region plays it mid-region (the lane is a timeline: unlike one-shot clips, whose launch resumes nothing from before it; sampler.md).
- **Not built (later, or decided elsewhere)**: stretching (`stretch: :beats` with LoopPlayer's slice stretch at markers did not fit cheaply: a region's audio would need restretching per tempo with seeks into the stretched copy; ACID's loop takes, which repeat and stretch to the project tempo, map onto `sample.follow` for whole-loop events and need a stretched source per region otherwise: left to the import work), length-keeping pitch (ACID's; `transpose:` is varispeed), Sample-level fades and loop points (regions play the Sample's data in play order with its level; its `fade` view isn't applied), per-region pan, envelopes/automation (below), editing operations and a view (out of scope).

## Measurements (i9-9880H container, YJIT, thread CPU time, 2026-11-04)

16 one-bar regions every quarter bar (4 sounding at a time, each with 1/16-bar smooth fade-in and fast fade-out) against the same audio as 4 `sample.follow` loops and as 16 notes of a one-shot sampler clip on 4 voices (`/claude/jobs/e84fa5c8/tmp/regions-1/bench/lane_bench.rb`, not in git: 2 s warm-up, 6 s measured, the Session's mix included; alternating runs with a master-ai e5e16542 worktree for the two existing kinds), ns per sample:

| case | 128-frame buffers | 800-frame buffers |
|---|---|---|
| empty Session (`0.constant`; the mix's own cost) | 76-90 | 16-17 |
| region lane (branch) | 355-358 | 40-66 |
| 4 `sample.follow` loops, master-ai / branch | 867-956 / 894-975 | 368-385 / 404-418 |
| one-shot sampler clip on 4 voices, master-ai / branch | 1197-1269 / 1204-1273 | 416-430 / 414-518 |

(3 alternating rounds each.)  The lane costs about a third of the sampler clip at 128 frames and a tenth at 800 (steady regions are one `Kernels.accumulate` each; the Ruby bookkeeping per region per block dominates at 128).  The follow loops ran 5-10% slower on the branch at 800 frames in two of three bench runs and not at 128; nothing on their path changed (LoopPlayer, Session), so it is not attributable (code layout or load; the Mac container had other sessions' work).

Allocations per 128-frame buffer (render_allocations_spec-style, `AllocationBudget.measure`, 400 buffers, the Session included): the lane 44.9 objects (7.2 kB; first version 136.2: per-buffer Rationals for region ends and Numo temporaries for every read; now steady regions add through `Kernels.accumulate` and allocate nothing, and the objects left are the fades' arrays), the follow loops 287 objects (61 kB), the sampler clip 34.0 (3.5 kB; master-ai 32.5).

## Found on the way

- Numo's `a.inplace + b` with `b` of a wider type (an SFloat output plus a DFloat product) isn't in place: it returns a new DFloat and leaves `a` alone, silently.  The first fade code added fade frames that way and lost them (zeros during every fade).  Products now go through `Kernels.accumulate` (SFloat factors) or are cast to SFloat first.

## Breakpoint automation (brief item 4: not built, a design question)

What exists: `turn :x, 0 => a, 1.bar => b, curve:` (`Gesture`: keyframes after a launch point, a monotone cubic or one Curve for every segment, exact frames) is a change that lands once and ramps a knob; a seek doesn't chase it (a seek back before it leaves the knob where the ramp left it; changes queued after a backward seek's landing land when the timeline reaches them again: arrangement.md, Seeks).  `turn :x, node` makes a knob follow a node, but followed nodes keep their own time across seeks.  Clip control lanes (`events(..., controls: { cc74: { points:, curve: :cubic } })`) are breakpoint CC automation on clips that seeks and launches chase (`Clip#control_chase`), with :step/:linear/:cubic for the whole lane.  Tweens take a curve per step since tween-curves-1 (`tween(values, steps, curve: [:fast, :hold, ...])`, curves.md): breakpoints with per-segment curves on the clip timeline (chased exactly on launches and seeks, Chase#index), but as keyframes of a looping or one-shot clip of values, not points at timeline positions.  So a lane of (position, value, curve-per-segment) points that drives a param and is chased exactly on seeks needs one of: (a) an `automation(...)` TimelineNode valuing its points by timeline position (exact on seeks by construction, like RegionPlayer), used as a param input (`cutoff: lane`) or followed by a knob whose follow restarts with the timeline (a change to Knob follow semantics); (b) clip control lanes growing per-segment curves (CC-resolution values, chased today); (c) Gestures compiled to scheduled turns at bar times (no seek chase, so not this).  Recommendation (a), with (b) for imported CC envelopes.
