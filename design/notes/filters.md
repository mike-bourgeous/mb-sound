# Filter System

Detailed notes moved from CLAUDE.md (2026-10-10); CLAUDE.md keeps a summary and points here.  Update both when behavior changes.


`lib/mb/sound/filter/` contains 16+ filter types (Biquad, FIR, Butterworth, Hilbert, Delay, etc.). Filters implement `#process` / `#reset`. `Filter::Cookbook` provides standard designs (lowpass, highpass, bandpass, etc.).

Graph cookbook filters are state-variable filters (user decision, 2026-10-08): `node.filter(:lowpass, cutoff:, quality:, gain:)`, `filter(150.hz.highpass(quality: 4))`, `filter(800)`, `peq`, `peq_series`, and `bandpass_series` build `Filter::SVF` (`lib/mb/sound/filter/svf.rb`; Simper's linear trapezoidal SVF, kernel `FastFilter.svf` in `fast_filter` sharing `mb_tan_pade` with the four-pole, exact Ruby mirror `SVF.process_ruby`) with the cookbook's types and parameter meanings (plus `:bandpass_skirt`, peak gain Q, in both structures), so static responses match the biquad (analytic within ~1e-8, float32 renders usually bit-identical).  Its states are integrator states, so cutoff/Q/gain may move on any sample: a cutoff diving toward 0 Hz gives no DC bump (the biquad's 10 ms DC reached 4.8 at Q 3 in the old linear elastic tween), and audio-rate cutoff FM stays bounded (the biquad's output went to infinity at a 2 kHz wobble, Q 4).  `gain:` may be a node with the SVF.  `structure: :biquad` (the only switch; the user declined a global one) builds the old `Filter::Cookbook` through the same path as before (kept for its thumps, e.g. drums); raw `Filter::Biquad`/`Cookbook` objects used directly are unchanged.  ns/sample (kernels, 512-sample buffers): constant params SVF 14 vs `dynamic_biquad` 32 (it recomputes cos/sin every sample), modulated 25 vs 28, scalar static 14 vs `Biquad#process` 12.  Measurements and renders: `/app/tmp/listening/svf_default/`.

`sig.lp4(cutoff, resonance: 0..1)` (aliases `four_pole`, `lowpass4`; also `filter(:lp4, cutoff:, resonance:)`) is a CEM3379-style 4-pole lowpass (`GraphNode::FourPole` over `Filter::FourPole`; zero-delay-feedback OTA cascade in the `fast_filter` extension, exact Ruby mirror `FourPole.process_ruby`, own tan/tanh/2^x approximations): cutoff (Hz, a Pitch, or a node such as `v.cutoff(...)`) and resonance read per sample; -12 dB at the cutoff, 24 dB/octave; like the SQ-80 it never self-oscillates by default (resonance 1 = k 3.9, +37 dB peak) and keeps its bass (-6 dB at full resonance, `compensation: 0.375`; 0 gives -14 dB); `self_oscillate: true` (its own curve, `FourPole.self_oscillate_gain`, kernel curves 2/3: the chosen curve compressed into 0..0.9 reaching k 4 at 0.9, then k = 4 + 1 × x² up to `SELF_OSCILLATE_K` 5, so oscillation starts at 0.9 and its level grows about linearly to ~0.22 peak; starts from silence; was k 4.3 with onset 0.74 until 2026-10-07), `mode:` :lp2/:bp2/:bp4/:hp2/:hp4 taps.  `resonance_curve: :db` (default since 2026-10-07; `:linear` is the round-1 k = r × k_max) makes the gain at the cutoff linear in dB (-12 to +33.8 dB, closed form `FourPole.resonance_curve`; peak +14.5 dB at 0.5 vs +7.5 linear; user-approved 2026-10-07).  Retune values written for the linear curve with `FourPole.db_resonance(linear)` (same loop gain; 0.3 -> 0.21, 0.5 -> 0.33, 0.7 -> 0.47, 0.75 -> 0.51, 0.9 -> 0.68, 0.95 -> 0.79; inverse `linear_resonance`).  Resonant sweeps of a low saw step through single harmonics while the cutoff is low (the narrow peak, not a glitch; detuned pairs make it irregular).  `drive:` (level; saturates above ~1/drive) with `drive_mode:` `:input` (default, tanh on the cascade input), `:stages` (every OTA stage's current plus the input, Zavalishin's one-step secant method), or `:feedback` (`clip: :soft`/`:hard`; only the resonance feedback y4 - c x clips, the MS-20's diode-clipper idea on our 4-pole, passband clean); the last two default to drive 1.  `quality:` instead of `resonance:` (a number or node, e.g. `v.quality(4)`) maps a 2-pole Q to the same gain at the cutoff; in voices `v.reso(0.6)` (alias `resonance_amount`) follows CC 71 (base at 64, 0 at 0, 1 at 127; `v.resonance` is the raw CC 71 Q multiplier).  ns/sample: linear 20, :input 28, :feedback 34-37, :stages 61 (+22 with a per-sample resonance node on the dB curve); ~0.16% of realtime per mono filter at 128-sample buffers.  Renders and measurements: `/app/tmp/listening/four_pole/` (README rounds 1 and 2).

`sig.diode(cutoff, resonance:)` (alias `diode_ladder`; `lp4(mode: :diode)`, `filter(:diode, ...)`) is a TB-303-style diode ladder (`FastFilter.diode_ladder`, exact mirror `FourPole.diode_process_ruby`; from the `research-classic-synths` prototype): four coupled one-pole stages (tridiagonal, open loop 1/D(s), D = s⁴ + 7s³ + 15s² + 10s + 1), TPT solved per sample (Thomas algorithm, linear in the loop input, so cutoff and resonance move every sample), oscillating at k = 901/49 = 18.39 at the cutoff.  Same knobs as lp4 (user: consistency): lp4's curves scaled by K/4 (same distance from oscillation per knob position, same `self_oscillate` onset 0.9), its own `:db` curve (gain at the peak frequency -25.3 to +32.3 dB, linear in dB), compensation, drive `:input`/`:feedback` (`:stages` raises).  `normalize: true` (default; user decision 2026-10-09: easy switching between lp4 and diode, not hardware fidelity; kernel argument 12) matches it to lp4 at the same settings: the stages' frequency × `FourPole.diode_cutoff_scale(k)` (m = 1 + 1.8585 (1 - x)/(1 + 2.1x + 10.6x²), x = k/K: 2.86 at r 0, 2.58 at 0.1, 2.14 at 0.2, 1.65 at 0.3, 1.31 at 0.4, 1.06 at 0.6, 1 at the oscillation edge) keeps the -12 dB-below-DC point at lp4's at r 0 (-12 dB at the cutoff, -3 dB at 0.28 × it vs lp4's 0.43; slopes ~8/11/16/21 dB/oct vs 16/21/24, brighter above) and puts the resonant peak at lp4's frequency from r 0.3 up (within 30 cents; round 3, 2026-10-09: the user heard lp4 a bit brighter at r 0.1-0.4, where round 2 matched the -12 dB point (b 14.4, c 4.85) and the diode's broader peak sat up to 450 cents low; now its -12 dB point is 0.3-0.45 octaves above lp4's at r 0.1-0.3, and a drive-1.5 saw sweep's 0.5-2 kHz bands are 1-5.5 dB under lp4's instead of 3-8.5 at r 0.1-0.4); `diode_compensation(k, k4, c)` replaces the compensation c by c' = ((1 + k)(1 + c k4)/(1 + k4) - 1)/k (k4 = `lp4_loop_gain` at the same knob), so the DC gain equals lp4's at every resonance, compensation, and curve (round 3: the plain c left the diode 1-2 dB quieter from r 0.1 up, -7.8 dB of bass at full resonance vs lp4's -6; now fundamentals within 0.3 dB, RMS within 0.5, and the resonant harmonic under drive 1.5 is still 14 dB over lp4's at r 0.9: acid stays acidic); and `diode_headroom(k)` (1 + (K/4 - 1) x²) lifts the input saturator so self-oscillation matches lp4's level within 0.02 dB (it was 13.25 dB under: the ladder passes 1/K at the resonant frequency, lp4 1/4; `:feedback` drive matched already); `quality:` then gives lp4's knob.  DC through self-oscillation (round 3 check of a swell 0.8→1 that looked offset): running means over whole input periods stay under 5e-4 for both filters (sub-5 Hz power -63 dB re total vs lp4's -50); the waveform's peaks are asymmetric (max/min midpoint up to -0.1, lp4 alike), not offset.  All three are computed only on resonance changes (a per-sample resonance node: ~98 ns/sample vs 91 in round 2, 113 vs 93 with the cutoff moving too).  `normalize: false` is the round-1 ladder (peak at the cutoff at every resonance: -25.3 dB there at r 0, slopes ~14/18/22, self-oscillation ~0.048 peak, plain compensation: -7.8 dB of bass at full resonance).  ns/sample (lp4 vs diode): :input constant 29 vs 34, cutoff per sample 33 vs 48.  Renders and measurements: `/app/tmp/listening/acid/` (round 2 in `round2/`, round 3 in `round3/` with its measurement scripts).

`sig.formant(*vowels, voice: :tenor, morph: 0, shift: 0, width: 1, normalize: true)` (alias `vowel`; `GraphNode::Formant`, 2026-10-11, sound-design-1) is a formant filter: five parallel SVF bandpasses (the `:bandpass` design of `Filter::SVF`, 0 dB peak times the formant's level) at a sung vowel's formants, from the Csound manual's formant table (https://csound.com/docs/manual/MiscFormants.html; voices `:soprano`, `:alto`, `:countertenor`, `:tenor`, `:bass`, vowels `:a :e :i :o :u`; `Formant::VOICES`), or custom vowels as five `[Hz, dB, bandwidth Hz]` triples.  With several vowels `morph:` (0..1 across the list, clamped; a number or node) interpolates each section's log frequency, log Q, and log gain between the two neighboring vowels; `shift:` (semitones, an Interval, or a node: key tracking with `v.number - 60`) multiplies every frequency keeping Q; `width:` (number or node, clamped at 0.01) scales bandwidths.  `normalize: true` scales each vowel so equal-power-per-octave input over 100 Hz-10 kHz keeps its power (computed from the sections' analytic responses at construction), and divides the gain by sqrt(width) (power follows bandwidth): a 110 Hz saw through every voice and vowel comes out -4.7 to +1.8 dB from its input RMS; `normalize: false` plays the table's levels (F1 at 0 dB, much quieter).  One kernel call per buffer, `FastFilter.formant` (in `fast_filter`, sharing `mb_svf.h`; exact Ruby mirror `Formant.process_ruby`): settings are recomputed only on samples where morph, shift, or width change (15 exp + 5 tan per change); same output at any buffer size; a plan boundary.  Cost over its input: constant settings ~20-27 ns/sample (0.1% of realtime at 128/800-sample buffers), every setting moving per sample ~160-170 ns/sample (0.8%, the worst case).  Before the kernel, five `Filter::SVF#dynamic_process` calls per buffer plus Numo morph math cost 220 ns/sample constant and ~900 with a moving morph at 128-sample buffers.

Denormal flushing (fixes-7, 2026-10-13, maintainer: "on the 800 grid instead of per buffer"): the SVF (`FastFilter.svf`, the plan's SVF and lane-major SVF, loop SVFs in `fast_loop`), the four-pole and diode ladder (`mb_four_pole_run`/`mb_diode_ladder_run`, the plan's and the lane-major kernels), and the formant filter flush states below 1e-30 (or not finite) to zero right after every 800th sample of their own time (`MB_FLUSH_GRID`/`mb_flush_value`/`mb_flush_count_read` in `mb_svf.h`, `mb_fp_flush_tick`; mirrors `Filter::SVF::FLUSH_GRID`, `.flush_count`, `.flush_value`), counting samples in an extra state entry (SVF `[ic1, ic2, count]`, four-pole `[s0..s3, count]`, formant's DFloat entry 10; states without it start counting at 0).  Before, they flushed at the end of each buffer (loop SVFs after every sample), so tails below 1e-30 differed by buffer size (a persona take's float stems, -590 dB).  For a run of 800-sample buffers starting with the filter it flushes where it did before.  Cost: SVF 15.7 -> 16.7 ns/sample at 800-sample buffers, four-pole, diode, and formant within noise.
