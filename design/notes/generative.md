# Generative music

The generative layer, phase G1 (branch `gen-1`, 2026-10-15; follow-ups `gen-2`) and G2, phrase memory (branch `gen-3`, 2026-10-16), built to the proposal of the research spike `research-generative` (commit be0a9e79, `research/generative/README.md` and `proposal.md`; never merged).  CLAUDE.md keeps a summary and points here.  Update both when behavior changes.

## The shape

A generator is a **looping Clip whose events for each cycle are a pure function of (seed, cycle)**.  Every random choice is a counter-based draw (`dice.rand(:note, i)`: a hash of the seed and the keys), never a call on a stateful `Random`, so cycle 517 comes out the same computed cold (a seek, a take replayed from the middle, a render that starts at hour three) or after cycles 0..516.  Generator clips carry their per-cycle block as the clip's `Clip::Variation` (the hook `humanize(vary:)` and `permute(vary:)` already used), so they play wherever clips play: synths, `tr808`/`tr909`/kits, scenes, `swap`, `rec`, `bake`, MIDI export, takes, graph documents.

Maintainer, 2026-10-15: "Start G1 now"; on the proposal's four design choices, "Accept for now but discuss later after build":

1. Generators are Clips first (a stateful live MIDI source for call and response comes later, phase 5).
2. Generation runs one cycle ahead on the scheduler thread, with the dice in C.
3. Knobs are read at a decision point one beat before each cycle (per-generator `lead:`) — **G3, not built yet**: G1 and G2 generators take numbers and lambdas of the cycle.

Maintainer, 2026-10-16: "Start G2 now": phrase memory (motifs, a a b a forms, answers, mutation every few passes) on the G1 dice, a same-register melody A/B, and dense/granular blur demos (the proposal's G3 phrase memory moved up; knob steering stays G3, long form later).  Listening Bench verdicts that shaped it: the spike's "Phrase memory vs a fresh walk" went to "a2 phrases, no blur" (so the demo plays no blur by default); G1's "melody(scale) vs melody(harmony)" went to the old lead, with "New is higher so comparison isn't as easy" (so every demo lead now plays in one register); G1 blur: "much more audible with a much higher density or a granular sampler" (`bin/songs/generative_blur.rb`).
4. Forms allow the same section name with different lengths — **G3/G4, not built**.

## Pieces (`lib/mb/sound/generative.rb`, `generative/`, `generative_methods.rb`)

- **Dice** (`generative/dice.rb`, kernel `ext/mb/sound/fast_generative`): state = splitmix64's finalizer of the seed; each key folds in as `mix(state ^ key)`.  Keys: Integers (low 64 bits, two's complement), Symbols and Strings (FNV-1a; `Symbol#hash` changes per process), Rationals (`mix(key(num)) ^ key(den)`), nil; Floats raise (draws must not depend on rounding).  `Dice.new(seed, *keys)`, `dice[:kick]` (a sub-path: `dice[:a].rand(1) == dice.rand(:a, 1)`), `rand`, `rands(n)` (one C call), `chance(p, ...)`, `int(range, ...)`, `pick(Array | Hash | Field, ...)`, `smooth(t, ...)` (value noise), `shuffle`.  `Dice.index(weights, u)` is the weighted pick everything uses: Array#sum's Kahan-Babuska total, then the first running sum past u * total (the C version reproduces Array#sum exactly).  `Dice.mode = :ruby` / `MB_SOUND_DICE=ruby` runs the exact mirror; `spec/ext/mb/sound/fast_generative_spec.rb` compares them.
- **Weights** (`weights.rb`): 12 pitch-class weights, a value (frozen), not a signal node, so operators are value arithmetic: `+` layers (or adds a number), `*` scales or masks class by class, `-` (floored at 0), `/`, `**` (sharpen), `blur(amount, reach:)` (leaks to neighbors, keeping the total), `blend(other, t)` (each side normalized, so halfway between keys favors the common tones; with a Harmony, a Harmony, gen-3), `floor`, `normalize`, `over(range, around:, width:)` → Field.  With a Harmony operand, `+`, `-`, `*`, and `blend` (gen-3; maintainer's open item, "my call, do it") return a Harmony (`Harmony.of_weights`: the Weights combined with the harmony at each moment, printing back as written, repeating with the harmony's period; gen-2): `s.blur(0.2) + chords * 2` works like `chords * 2 + s.blur(0.2)`, bit for bit.  Made by `scale(:dorian, :d).weights` (`Scale#weights(*per_degree)`, a module included into Scale), `tones(D3, F3, A3)` (a chord layer), `chord(:min7, root: :d).weights` (a named chord layer, below), `weights(12 numbers)`.  `puts w` is a bar chart.
- **Chord** (`chord.rb`, gen-2; maintainer 2026-10-15: "Add named chords too"): `chord(:min7, root: :d)` is a chord value; `.weights` is the same layer as `tones(*chord.notes)`, `.weights(2, 1, 1.5, 1)` weighs each chord tone from the root up (cycled), `.notes` gives Notes lowest first after `inversion:` (0 up to size - 1).  `SequenceMethods#chord` dispatches: one quality Symbol/String plus `root:` (no `scale:`) is the value, every other call the MIDI transform as before; `Transform::Chord` now raises for a chord name with `root:`/`scale:` (it ignored them), so the two never overlap.  Qualities are the transform's `SHAPES`/`ALIASES` (one table; `:maj :min :dim :aug :sus2 :sus4 :'7'/:dom7 :maj7 :min7/:m7 :m7b5 :dim7 :'6' :min6/:m6 :'9' :maj9 :min9 :add9 :power ...`).  Roots: names (octave 4 without a number, like `scale`; `:d3`, `:bb2`), Notes, note numbers, Pitches (nearest note).  Prints back as `chord(:min7, root: :d).weights`; `GraphIO::Registry::VALUES` allows `Chord#weights` (`chord` is the recorded DSL method).  `Generative.weights` takes Chords, so harmony keyframes may be chords.
- **Field** (`field.rb`): weights over a contiguous range of note numbers (pitch class × a Gaussian bell around `around:` with `width:` semitones), plus `harmonic` (the class weights inside the bell's 5% register) for `tonal` (the scale notes in register), `home(near)`, `near(target, u)`.  `draw(u)`, `**`.
- **Harmony** (`harmony.rb`): keyframes at clip positions (Durations: `12.bars`, `3.beats`; 0 for the start) of Weights, Scales, Chords, or note Arrays, blended between (`curve: :linear`, `:step`, or any Curve), `loop:`; the blend's shape is Weights#blend's (each side normalized) and its total moves from one keyframe's total to the next's (gen-3: G1 dropped to a total of 1 between keyframes and jumped back to the keyframe as written on its exact position, so `key + chords * 1.5` weighed the scale notes at 1/11 of the chord tones between keyframes and 1/2.5 on them; found when motifs lost the scale notes between keyframes; changes what G1 melodies and strikes draw from layered harmonies, A/B `g2.hfix` on the Bench); `blur` and operators apply to the blend at each moment, and may take other Harmonies (`key.blur(0.15) + chords * 1.5`).  `at(position)` caches by position (512) and caches held keyframes with their operators applied; `over(...)` gives a **FieldTimeline** (`at(position)` → Field, its bell computed once, Fields cached by their weights).  `harmony(0 => a, 12.bars => b)` parses as keywords with non-Symbol keys (`**positions`).  Keys and `loop:` must be lengths (gen-2; maintainer 2026-10-15: "Require lengths"): any zero is the start, other plain numbers raise with a `write 12.bars` hint (G1 read them as bars; live mode still does, with a warning, like bare dB numbers), seconds lengths raise (they don't follow the tempo).
- Every value remembers the expression that made it (`Generative::Value`) and prints it back: `scale(:dorian, D4).weights.blur(0.1) + tones(D3, F3, A3, C4) * 1.2`, parenthesizing equal-precedence right operands so values rebuild with the same rounding.  The recipe writer prints them through the same code (`RubyWriter#leaf`).
- **Generator** (`generator.rb`, `producer.rb`): the Variation block of a generator clip: `events(cycle)` computes once per cycle (a Mutex-guarded cache of 32 cycles), normalizes rows (`[start, length, value, velocity, { marks }]`, starts in exact whole notes or Durations; Floats raise) and checks `reach` (latest end from the cycle's start, 2 cycles by default; it sets the clip's note-off lookback), and keeps `stats` (cycles, notes, thread CPU, worst cycle).  `Producer` marks variations that replace events: `Clip#map_clip` (transpose, legato, reverse, stretch, rotate, vel, ...) wraps them with its map (`Mapped`), so transforming a generator transforms what it generates (before G1, `melody(vary: true).transpose(12)` silently played untransposed variations).
- **Ahead** (`ahead.rb`): reading cycle N queues N+1 on a runner: `Session::Scheduler#background(&block)` on a realtime session (the scheduler thread, priority -3; the scheduler attaches itself when its thread starts and detaches on close).  Renders and specs have no runner: cycles are computed where they're read, with the same events.
- **Clip.generate** (`clip_generate.rb`, console `generate`): `generate(1.bar, values: notes, seed:, reach:) { |cycle, dice| rows }`.  Cycle lengths must be lengths (`2.bars`, `1r/2`): a plain Integer raises (it would be a note division, the spike's friction 2).  `dice` is `Dice.new(seed, cycle)`; `Dice.new(dice.seed)` for draws shared across cycles.  The clip's own events are cycle 0's (what it plays without looping, what listings and `first_note` see).
- **Clip#values**: the notes a clip may play (`values:` for generator clips, else its events' values); drum kits (`Drums::Machine`, `TR808`, `Kit#plays_clip?`) choose voices from it instead of base events (the spike's placeholder events are gone).
- **strikes** (`strikes.rb`, console): candidates on each step of `every:` with `chance:` (a number or a lambda of the cycle), keyed by the **global** step, so the cycle `length:` only sets caching; a candidate within `gap:` steps after another candidate is dropped (a look-back rule: any cycle needs only `gap - 1` steps before it); the note is drawn from the field at the strike's position, sharpened by `sharpen:`.
- **motifs** (`motifs.rb`, `GenerativeMethods#motifs`; G2, gen-3): a melody with phrase memory.  `motifs(source, steps = 8, step: 1.n8, form: %i[a a b a], answer: { b: :a }, density: 0.5, vary: 0.2, mutate: 0.15, evolve: 2, leap: 3, range:, around:, width:, velocity:, legato:, seed:)` is a generator clip of one phrase per cycle (`steps * step` long): cycle n plays letter `form[n % form.length]` (nil: a silent phrase) of pass `n / form.length`, motif generation `pass / evolve`.  Named `motifs`, not the proposal's `phrases`: `phrases 8, 16` is a Genre::Builder method (phrase lengths); no MB::Sound, Pry, Clip, or GraphNode name collides.
  - A motif (`Motifs::Motif`) is per grid step: whether a note starts there (step 0 always; others with `density`, strong (even) steps 1.2x, weak 0.8x), a contour in scale steps (moves of 0, ±1, ±2, ±3 weighted 1, 3, 1, 0.4, up to `leap`, turning back past ±6 from the first note, then centered so the notes average out at 0), a velocity, and a per-letter anchor offset of -1..1 steps.  Realized per note against the field where the note starts: the scale notes there (`Field#tonal`, cached per Field), the anchor the scale note nearest `around:` (the range's middle by default) plus the offset, the note `contour` steps from it.  So a motif comes back through chord and key changes, in the register `range:`/`around:` set (to match another line: the demo's leads average 62-63 at seed 7).
  - Rules: a phrase's first note is the strongest note nearest its target (`Field#home`: a chord tone when a chord layer is on top); answers (`answer: { b: :a }`) keep the call's rhythm and the first half of its contour, mirror the rest around the last kept note, land on the strongest note nearest the end (home), and hold it to the phrase's end; a call's last note moves a scale step off the strongest notes (an open ending) when the field has stronger and weaker notes; repeats of a letter within a pass vary (`vary`: a middle note moved a step or dropped, the note before holding through); blurred wrong notes (a semitone neighbor outside the scale notes, by its weight, the target counted 3 times) only in the middle of a phrase.  A Scale source gets its root weighted 1.5 so answers have a home; Weights and Harmonies weigh their own.  Answers answer motifs only (chains raise).
  - Mutation, bounded (so a cold jump costs one cycle and a motif keeps its face): each step of a motif mutates in a generation with probability `mutate`, and its state is set by its latest mutation within `Motifs::MEMORY` (32) generations, relative to the first form: restore (weight 3), neighbor (±1 step, 3), toggle (drop a note, or fill a rest with a passing note between its neighbors, 1.5), accent (×0.8 or ×1.15, 1).  A motif is never more than one change per step from where it began, and generation g costs one look back per step (draws come 64 generations per C call, cached), whatever g is.  The spike's `Phrases` mutated generation g from g - 1 (a memo chain: 20-45 ms cold at cycle 10,000, more further in); this definition is a pure function of (seed, letter, g), so sequential and cold are the same by construction.  (A motif can't drift arbitrarily far: question for the maintainer whether a slower "the tune grows" drift is wanted, e.g. re-basing every N generations, at the cost of chains.)
  - Recipes and documents: a recorded GenerativeMethods call; `form:` prints as an Array of Symbols, `answer:` as a Hash.  The loader counts `steps` like melody's; motifs itself caps steps at 256 and the form at 64 letters.
- **melody** (`SequenceMethods#melody`, `generative/melody.rb`): with a Scale, the same notes as before G1 (`vary: true` is now a generator clip; each cycle still draws from `Random.new(Clip.cycle_seed(seed, cycle))`, and the walk's pick goes through `Dice.index`, the same arithmetic: 0 mismatches over 1000 seeds × parameter sets; `bin/songs/midi_transforms_song.rb`, whose bass is a `melody(vary: true)`, renders byte-identical on master-ai and gen-1).  With Weights, a Harmony, a Field, or a FieldTimeline: a walk over the notes with weight (`leap:` of them at most), by weight / move size, from the strongest note nearest `start:`, reading the field where each note starts; counter-based dice.

## Seeds, takes, documents

- Seeds default to `MB::Sound.next_seed` (recorded as drawn seeds, `seed: drawn_seed(N)` in documents).  Takes replay generator tracks bit for bit at other buffer sizes, through scene changes, transposed generators, and seeks (`generator_spec.rb` "takes").
- Graph documents: `tones`, `weights`, `harmony` are top-level values (`Registry::TOP_EXTRA`); `Scale#weights` and the Weights/Harmony/Field methods are in `Registry::VALUES`; `strikes`/`melody`/`generate` are recorded DSL calls.  `generate` blocks print as their source and load with `trust: :code` (or a `named_proc`).  Limits: `LIMITS[:steps]` (4096) for melody counts; strikes check 4096 steps per cycle; Fields 128 notes; blur reach 1..6.
- `Clip#event_at` (chases after seeks and launches) now takes the previous cycle's last note before a cycle's first note (it took the same cycle's last note, which differs for varying clips).

## CPU (i9-9880H container, Ruby without YJIT, 2000 cycles each from a fresh generator, thread CPU)

| generator | notes/cycle | mean | p99 | worst | per note | cold at cycle 10^6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| melody(Scale, 8, vary: true) | 8 | 82 us | 315 us | 1.2 ms | 10.3 us | 77 us |
| melody(harmony, 8, vary: true) | 8 | 140 us | 401 us | 0.8 ms | 17.5 us | 126 us |
| melody(harmony, 32 sixteenths) | 32 | 536 us | 1.2 ms | 2.1 ms | 16.7 us | 935 us |
| the demo's lead (key.blur + chords) | 5 | 92 us | 311 us | 1.6 ms | 18.4 us | 83 us |
| **worst case**: melody, 32 sixteenths, a 4000-bar blend (every note a new blend position) | 32 | 1.34 ms | 2.7 ms | 10.4 ms | 41.8 us | 454 us |
| strikes(harmony.over, 1.n8) | 1.9 | 44 us | 155 us | 1.0 ms | 22.6 us | 58 us |
| strikes(1.n16, chance 0.9, gap 1) | 14.4 | 211 us | 1.0 ms | 1.5 ms | 14.6 us | 253 us |
| generate (16 rows × 16 steps) | 77 | 358 us | 1.0 ms | 2.5 ms | 4.6 us | 421 us |

- Cost per cycle is bounded by the generator's shape (slots × leap, steps × gap, rows × steps), never by search or retries; there are no memo chains in G1, so a cold jump costs one cycle.  The worst single cycles (up to ~10 ms) are GC pauses and first calls; the worst steady cost is a harmony's first visit to a blend position (blend + operators + a Field, ~35-40 us), cached after that, so a looping harmony pays it once per loop position (harmonies combined with operators cache too when they all loop with the same length, `Harmony#period`).
- A dice draw is ~0.3-0.5 us from Ruby (the C hash is a few ns; the rest is the method call), against ~150 us per note with the spike's Ruby-only dice.
- Live, cycles are computed a cycle ahead on the scheduler thread, so the render thread reads cached cycles; the first cycle after a launch or a cold seek is computed where it's read (one cycle's cost, above).
- Renders: the 32-bar demo (92 s) renders in ~33 s wall (2.8x realtime, instruments and rooms included).

G2 (gen-3, 2026-10-16, same container, Ruby without YJIT, 2000 cycles each from a fresh generator, thread CPU; the machine was busier than G1's run: load average ~1.8 from other sessions; G1's melody(harmony, 8) reran at 24 us per note against 17.5 above, so compare within this table):

| generator | notes/cycle | mean | p99 | worst | per note | cold at cycle 10^6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| melody(harmony, 8, vary: true) (G1, rerun) | 8.0 | 194 us | 830 us | 14.1 ms | 24.2 us | 901 us |
| the demo lead: motifs(key + chords, 8 eighths, a a b a) | 5.0 | 125 us | 744 us | 1.9 ms | 25.2 us | 119 us |
| motifs(16 sixteenths, mutate 0.3, evolve 1) | 8.5 | 213 us | 1097 us | 2.5 ms | 25.0 us | 618 us |
| motifs(32 sixteenths, density 0.9, form of 8 letters, evolve 1) | 23.6 | 429 us | 1291 us | 1.8 ms | 18.2 us | 560 us |
| **worst case**: motifs 64 x 1.n32, density 1, mutate 0.01 (full 32-generation look-backs), evolve 1, 4000-bar blend | 54.5 | 3479 us | 7212 us | 15.1 ms | 63.8 us | 4527 us |

- motifs cost about what melody costs per note (the field reads and Ruby event building dominate); per cycle it does one Field read and one scale lookup per note, plus, when a new generation starts, one look back of at most 32 cached draws per step.  The worst case (64 thirty-second steps, density 1, `mutate: 0.01` so every look back runs its full 32 generations, a new generation every pass, a 4000-bar blend so every note is a new blend position) is ~3.5 ms per 2-bar cycle, ~64 us per note, 3-4.5 ms cold at cycle 10^6 (two runs); the worst single cycles (up to ~15-19 ms) are GC pauses and first calls.  No memo chains: a cold jump costs one cycle.

## Not built yet (later phases)

- Evolving rhythms (`beats`, energy, holds, Euclidean mutation, groove velocity curves, fills): the proposal's G2, not yet scheduled (phrase memory went first, maintainer 2026-10-16).
- G3: knob steering at decision points (`Knob#value_at`, per-generator `lead:`), e.g. motifs' `vary`/`mutate`/`density` from knobs.
- G4: long form (`markov`, `lsystem`, `flow { generate ... }`, per-occurrence section lengths).
- (Fixed in midi-consol-1, 2026-10-16: `ghost_chords`, `permute` without `vary:`, `ratchet`, `roll`, and `truncate` now work per generated cycle through `Clip#map_cycles`; see sequences.md.)

## Demo and renders

`bin/songs/generative_demo.rb` (32 bars at 84 BPM: a key harmony blending D dorian ↔ F major with a stepped chord layer; a koto lead playing `motifs` (G2: a one-bar motif in the form a a b a, b answering a, mutating every 2 passes), rin from `strikes`, low bowls from `strikes`, a sine pad from a plain `generate` block; no blur by default since gen-3 (`-u 0.1` light); `-l melody` plays G1's melody on the probability scale and `-l old` the older `melody(vary: true)`, all three leads in one register (A3..A5, averaging near E4); `-s` the seed, `-p` one part).  `bin/songs/generative_blur.rb` (gen-3): blur you can hear, a 16-bar field played as a sixteenth-note line of independent draws (`-m dense`) or as a granular cloud whose grains start on a generator's note-ons and play at their pitches, one weighted draw per grain (`-m grains`; `trigger:`/`freq:` as `v.grains` uses them), `-u` the blur.  gen-blur-2 (2026-10-16, the maintainer's Bench notes on the 64ths, "some indication of the changes, maybe a 1-per-bar bassline?", and on the cloud, "much denser and more cloudy, with the transitions happening over a shorter span, and something solid and not blurred to anchor the changes"): `--bass`/`-B` (`bass: true`) adds an anchor, one note a bar (the chord's root two octaves under the line plus a quieter fifth, `generative_blur_bass_clip`, never blurred); `-c dense` (`cloud: :dense`) a cloud of about 40 grains a second (chance 0.9 per 128th) of 350 ms (about 14 overlapping) over a 3 s tone; `-t bars` (`transition:`) moves the key changes to bar 9 (F major, on the B flat chord) and bar 13 (D dorian, on the G7), each blended over the bars before it (0 a step).  All three are off by default: the earlier renders' commands render bit-identically (f32 md5 checked for `-m dense -u 0.15`, `-m grains -u 0.4`, `-m both -u 0.4`, `-n 64 -u 0.15`).  Renders: `/app/tmp/listening/generative-g1/` (Bench set `generative-g1-2026-10-15`) and `/app/tmp/listening/generative-g2/` (set `generative-g2-2026-10-16`, `render.sh` and `bench_set.py` there), `/app/tmp/listening/gen-blur-2/` (set `gen-blur-2-2026-10-16`).
