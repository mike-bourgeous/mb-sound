# Delays

<!-- summary -->
`#delay` (`Filter::Delay`) and `#multitap` share `MB::Sound::DelayLine` (C reads, exact mirrors; `:sinc` interpolation by default, `:cubic`, `:linear`); times are any length or node (`96.samples`, `3.n16`, `lfo.samples`), `feedback:`/`wet:`/`dry:` numbers or nodes, and delays ring out after their input ends (`MB::Sound::QuietRun#advance`).  Delay networks (`FastDelay.network`) build `multitap(t1, t2, feedback: [g1, g2] | g)`, `ping_pong(t, feedback:, spread:, mix:, input: :mono | :stereo)`, and `haas(15.ms, side:)`; tape echoes are `sig.tape_echo(:reel | :slapback | :ping_pong | :dub, time:, feedback:, mix:, tone:, low_cut:, drive:, wow:, flutter:)` (library family `:delay`).  Same output at any buffer size.

`Filter::Delay` (`#delay`) and `GraphNode::MultitapDelay` (`#multitap`) are front ends for one `MB::Sound::DelayLine` (`lib/mb/sound/delay_line.rb`): a circular buffer that grows without losing stored audio, read in C (`MB::Sound::FastDelay.read`) with a Ruby mirror (`#read_ruby`) that specs check for exactly equal values.  Interpolation (`interpolation:`) defaults to `:sinc` (`DelayLine::DEFAULT_INTERPOLATION`; Kaiser-windowed, cutoff lowered by the read speed when a moving delay raises the pitch, so no aliasing up to 4x); `:cubic` and `:linear` (the old lo-fi sound) are options.  Constant whole-sample delays read directly in every mode; a moving or fractional `:sinc` delay costs ~1.5-2% of realtime per mono delay.  No mode reads samples newer than the current input.  Delay times are any length (`0.01`, `250.ms`, `96.samples`, `3.n16`, nodes, `node.samples`) held as `Length::Source`, so they keep their unit when the sample rate changes and rate changes rebuild nothing; fractional constant delays are kept (snapped within 1e-9 of whole samples).  `feedback:`, `wet:`, and `dry:` may be numbers or graph nodes; `#delay` smooths delay changes by default (`smooth:`), `#multitap` only with `smooth:` (`smoothing:` is the older spelling, still accepted; 2026-10-13).  `delay(seconds: t)` is deprecated (warns once per call site; graph documents load it silently, and export prints `delay(t)`).  With a block (`sig.delay(t, feedback: g) { |fb| insert }`) the feedback runs through the block one sample at a time (see Feedback loops), compensated with `compensate: :echo` (low cuts and bandpasses count as wires, so echoes land on k * t).  Tools: `bin/delay_gallery.rb` (null-test cases), `bin/delay_benchmark.rb` (`--interpolation`), `bin/delay_quality.rb` (error and aliasing against an exact sine answer).

Ringing out (2026-10-13, branch fixes-2): `#delay` (with or without feedback), `#multitap`, and feedback loops (so the tape-echo form too) keep playing after their input ends, instead of ending with it (a one-shot pluck through `delay(1.n8.dotted, feedback: 0.5)` used to play nothing in a Session, whose tempo delay started at 0.375 s, after the pluck had ended).  The input's end (nil, or a short last buffer) feeds silence in; the node ends on the exact sample where what it has written into its delay lines (the feedback, or a loop's output) has stayed below -90 dB (`MB::Sound::QuietRun::QUIET`, the level every tail uses; the ring-out form of the one quiet-run engine since node-traits-1, which replaced `RingOut`) for its longest delay plus `DelayLine::MARGIN` (the sinc reach), so nothing louder can come out later and the tail has the same length at any buffer size.  A plain delay or multitap therefore ends one delay (+50 samples) after its input; what is cut is below -90 dB times wet/feedback.  `Filter::Delay#ring_out(count)` is the hook (called by `Filter::SampleWrapper` once its source ends, only for filters that define it and have no extra inputs); `MultitapDelay#frame_input` pads with silence; `FeedbackLoop` reads ended boundary inputs as silence (an ended modulator input reads as 0 too) and watches its output.  The tape form's dry mix and pipeline sum use Mixers with `stop_early: false` (`DelayMethods.ring_sum`) so the input's end doesn't cut the echoes.  `delay(t, ring_out: false)` ends with its input instead (Reverb's predelay uses it, since its `extra_time` already makes the tail).  A loop gain of 1 or more never decays, so the tail plays on (Session tails and `write` cap it).  Effects after a `Ringdown` (effect scripts) and graphs whose input never ends (clips, live input) are unchanged.  Also fixed: tempo-synced delays sampled outside a Session started at 0 samples and glided up (the start reset ran before the smoother existed).

Delay networks, ping-pong, Haas, tape echoes (delay-fx-1, 2026-10-28; A1 and a light A2 of the 2026-10-03 delay feedback proposal).  `MB::Sound::FastDelay.network` (fast_delay.c; `DelayLine.network`, exact Ruby mirror `DelayLine.network_ruby`) runs L real delay lines and T taps one sample at a time: every line is written with its input, every tap reads its line (as `#read` does: whole-sample delays directly unless sinc, the sinc read speed from the tap's previous delay), then each line adds `gain[line][tap] * tap` for every gain (numbers or per-sample buffers; in tap order, in double precision).  One line, one tap, and gains `[[g]]` were `DelayLine#feedback` bit for bit (until smoothing-1 removed it; both add in double precision).  `GraphNode::DelayNetwork` (MultiOutput: one `Tap` per tap; inputs per line, nodes or nil; taps `[line, time]` with any delay time and optional smoothing; gains numbers or nodes) builds on it; the first tap read in a buffer runs the network; output and ending are the same at any buffer size; after every input ended it rings out with `QuietRun#advance` over what the lines get back (no feedback: one longest delay plus `DelayLine::MARGIN`, as MultitapDelay).  A plan boundary; it declares long memory (`node_traits memory: :long`, NodeTraits; before node-traits-1 it wasn't a GraphNode, so `Session::Carry` counted it as carried and cut its echoes).
- `multitap(t1, t2, ..., feedback: [g1, g2, ...] | g)`: regenerating taps on one line (a tape echo with several playback heads).  An Array sets each head's regeneration (the maintainer, 2026-10-30: "Per head"); one gain is the loop's as a whole, shared evenly (each tap `g / taps`, so any gain below 1 decays); recipes and graph documents print either form back (spec'd: a loaded document plays the same samples); without `feedback:` the plain MultitapDelay (unchanged; zero gains give its samples and length exactly).
- `ping_pong(time = 1.n8, feedback: 0.5, spread: 1, dry: 1, wet: 1, mix: nil, input: :mono | :stereo, smooth: true, interpolation:)` (alias `pingpong`; `StereoDelayMethods`, GraphNode and Channels, not run per channel): two lines feeding each other (`[[0, g], [g, 0]]`); `:mono` (default) puts the input (a stereo input's average) into the left line, so echoes go L, R, L, ... each `feedback` times the last; `:stereo` feeds each side its own line; `spread:` is a `width` of the echoes (0 = every echo centered); `mix:` as for `#chorus`.
- `haas(time = 15.ms, side: :right, level: 1)`: one side delayed (precedence widening); stereo inputs keep the other side.
- `tape_echo(preset = :reel, time:, feedback:, mix:, tone:, low_cut:, drive:, wow:, flutter:, stereo: :mono | :ping_pong, spread:, seed:)` (`GraphNode::TapeEcho`, Presettable family `:delay`, `L.fx.delay.dub`; presets `:reel` (3/16, warm), `:slapback` (110 ms), `:ping_pong` (1/8, stereo), `:dub` (3/8, dark and saturated); the help effects topic plays `:dub`): a feedback loop `(input + g * fb).delay(time + wobble)` through a highpass (`low_cut:`), a lowpass (`tone:`), and `(x * drive).softclip / drive` on every pass; wow is a `drift` at 0.7 Hz and flutter a 6.5 Hz sine, both in ms, added to the delay time (Durations follow the tempo through `Sequence::TempoNode.seconds_source`); delay smoothing off (see below).  Every value setting takes a node (delays-2, 2026-10-31: knobs turn them without a rebuild): `tone:`/`low_cut:` nodes go into the loop's filters, a `drive:` node into `softclip(x * d) / d` with d at least `TapeEcho::MIN_LIVE_DRIVE` (1e-3: a drive node at 0 is nearly clean, -61 dB from the number 0's linear loop, the softclip's half sample), `wow:`/`flutter:` nodes scale unit-depth wobbles (constant nodes within -117 dB of the numbers, flutter's `sine.at(f)` vs `sine * f`; tone, low_cut, wow exact, drive -150 dB).  Loops use `compensate: :dc`: the `:pitch` mode measures the loop phase at 1 / time, a few Hz for an echo, where a highpass leads by up to 180 degrees, so repeats landed up to half an echo late (measured on master-ai 8c7c1c24: `delay(0.1, feedback: 0.5) { |fb| fb.filter(200.hz.highpass(quality: 0.5)).filter(3000.hz.lowpass(quality: 0.5)) }` put its first echo 2321 samples late, the second 2361 early; :dc within a few samples); `#delay`'s insert loops use `compensate: :echo` since omnibus16 (the same echo times as :dc, see feedback_loops.md; `bin/effects/tape_delay.rb`'s and feedback_song's echoes moved onto their times, Bench set omnibus16), and tape echoes keep `:dc`, cheaper while the wobbled time moves.  Echo peaks land within about 1 ms of k * time (the filters' shape).  `:ping_pong` is one loop of `2 * time` through two tape passes (right repeats; both wobbles in the one delay, so compensation stays exact) and a third pass outside the loop for the left repeats (a loop's inner nodes can't be read outside it).  Stereo inputs in `:mono` get two tapes (seeds `seed` and `seed + 1`).
- Found while building it (pre-existing, fixed in omnibus16): `#delay` with a node time and the default smoothing started the time at 0 and glided it up at the smoothing rate (a 0.375 s wobbled time read 1200-1600 samples in the first buffers), so a short input's loop rang out before its first echo; tape echoes pass `smooth: false`.  Since omnibus16 the smoother starts at the node's first value (`Filter::Delay#reset_delay` without samples marks a node time to seed from the next buffer's first sample; construction, `smoothing=`, and `reset` do it; `delay=` changing a running delay to a node still glides), so `delay(lfo)` starts on its time at any buffer size and rate.  What moved: the first ~15 ms of the ambient presets' wow delay (`ambient_tape`, `ambient_koto`, `ambient_harp`: 6-9 ms, read near 0 before), flanger.rb's first 0.19 s, `tape_delay.rb --pitch` throughout (the start goes around the loop), critters/wind_chimes/fifth_pad starts; Bench set omnibus16.

One engine for delay feedback (smoothing-1, 2026-10-30; arch-core finding 2): `delay(t, feedback: g)` without a block is the tape form with an identity insert, `feedback_delay(t, ...) { |d| d }`: a `FeedbackLoop` (fast_loop) around a `Filter::Delay` that has no feedback of its own, so plain feedback delays, insert delays, and `feedback { }` share one ring-out rule, one latency story, and one place for inserts.  `Filter::Delay`'s feedback (its `feedback:` option, `#feedback=`, the branch in `#process`), `DelayLine#feedback`/`#feedback_ruby`, and `FastDelay.feedback` are gone; `Filter::Delay.new(feedback:)` and `Duration#delay(feedback:)` raise (unknown keyword); `feedback: 0`/`false`/`nil` is a plain delay; complex numeric gains raise.  Samples: equal to the old kernel within float32 rounding (it added the feedback in double precision, loops round each op; max difference 1.8e-7 over 14 cases: dry/wet, dB, node gains, node and smoothed times, 96-sample and fractional cubic/linear delays, tempo delays, complex signals; plain gains on dry-free delays bit for bit); a one-shot's tail ends when the loop's output (not the feedback written back) has stayed below -90 dB, one delay later than before (the cut part is below -90 dB).  Complex signals (the spectral example above, `bin/delay_gallery.rb`'s complex case) loop their real and imaginary parts in two runs (see feedback_loops.md).  A delay with `feedback:` inside another loop raises `Plan::Unsupported` (a loop inside a loop).  `#inspect` shows `#<FeedbackLoop ... delay(t, feedback: g)>` (or the dry Mixer); recipes print the call unchanged.

Cost (thread CPU ns/sample, alternating with master-ai, 128 / 800-sample blocks, source `220.hz.saw` about 75 / 27): `delay(0.3, feedback: 0.5)` 158 -> 163 / 63 -> 47; `dry: 1` 184 -> 159 / 69 -> 48; a moving gain with `dry: 1` 205 -> 220 / 88 -> 57; a moving time (`0.7.hz.lfo.at(0.002..0.006)`, feedback 0.6, dry 1, default smoothing) 693 -> 401 / 527 -> 191; `ping_pong(0.2, feedback: 0.5)` (the slew limiter in C) 673 -> 347 at 800.  Two details keep 128-sample blocks at parity: numeric `wet:`/`dry:` are mixed into the loop's output in C (`FeedbackLoop#output_mix`, `FastArithmetic.wet_dry`; a Tee, a Multiplier, and a Mixer around the loop cost more than the loop at 128 samples), and a bare loop (the insert returns the delayed signal: plain `feedback:` or `{ |fb| fb }`) skips latency compensation (`FeedbackLoop#skip_compensation!`; nothing to compensate, bit for bit the same as `:echo` over constant, negative, sign-changing and moving gains and times), so no per-block pitch tracking.  graph_profile `--plan both`, best of 2, % of realtime at 128 / 800 (YJIT, same conditions): flanger.rb on arp_a7 28.5 / 24.0 -> 24.9 / 21.0, tape_delay.rb 14.0 / 11.7 -> 10.7 / 9.5, fm_chain.rb (a `wheel.smooth(0.05)`) on dense_modulated.mid 10.4 / 4.8 -> 8.4 / 3.6.
- Delay networks as loop programs (measured, not built): `multitap(0.11, 0.23, 0.37, feedback: 0.6).mixdown` (`FastDelay.network`) 291 -> 247 ns/sample at 800 (the smoother's C) against 129-146 for the same three regenerating taps written as one loop (`feedback { |fb, x| x + (fb.delay(0.11) + fb.delay(0.23) + fb.delay(0.37)) * 0.2 }`), 650 vs 482-553 at 128.  Not identical (the network adds its gains in double precision and outputs each tap; loops round each op to float32, and loop taps can't be read outside the loop yet), so `FastDelay.network` stays: a decision for later (the loop form would need loop taps for `multitap`'s per-tap outputs and `ping_pong`'s two sides).

Delay-time smoothing in C (smoothing-1): `Filter::LinearFollower#process` (the slew limiter `DelayLine.smoother` builds, also `clip_rate` and `Tone#follower`) runs in C for SFloat and DFloat data (`FastControl.slew`, `mb_slew_run` in `mb_smooth.h`; exact mirror `#process_ruby`, also for other inputs; in place exactly when the old map was).  The behavior is kept: the slew limit bounds the pitch a delay change makes (0.5 s/s: 0.5x to 1.5x while a time glides) and LFO-moving times under the limit pass untouched, so no delay changed sound.  (A smoother pitch corner, e.g. a short B-spline after the slew, was considered and not built: it would delay every moving time by half its length; a question for the maintainer.)

Idle checks (node-traits-1, 2026-10-30): `Filter::Delay`, `MultitapDelay`, `DelayNetwork`, and `FeedbackLoop` declare `node_traits memory: :long, idle_check: :sleep_ready?`: true when their lines hold nothing at -90 dB or above within the longest delay read so far (`DelayLine#holds_quiet?`), so idle Synth lanes and drum voices holding them sleep once the echoes have died away, and a looper holding its loop never does (graph_nodes.md, Node traits and quiet runs).  Re-applied in delay-sleep-1 (2026-10-30) after sleeping voices learned to catch their random streams up (graph_nodes.md, Node traits: `random:`), so the noise a voice draws doesn't depend on whether it slept.
