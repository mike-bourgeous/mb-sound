# MIDI as text: Emoji bytes

One pictograph per byte: byte `b` becomes U+1F300+b in the Miscellaneous Symbols and Pictographs block, which has exactly 256 assigned characters (0x00 = 🌀, 0x4D = 🍍, 0xFF = 🏿).  Newlines every 32 characters are not data.

Source: `Synth Demo 1.mid` by Mike Bourgeous (4411 bytes, format 1, 4 tracks, 960 ticks per quarter note).  This encoding: 17782 bytes of UTF-8, 4549 characters.
